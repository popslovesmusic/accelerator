## 3. Inferred Inside Framework
- Distinguishability is procedural rather than intrinsic in the current governance stack.
- The ontology does not require pre-existing identity for a condition to participate in relation.
- The campaign does not support a hidden object ontology claim under current rules.
- The main residual risk is terminological, not structural: some registry entries still use provisional object-style labels while simultaneously denying that admissible distinctions exist independently of conditioning.
- The retest is a negative result for promotion: it supports bounded basin survival and admissibility recovery, but it does not support the overall conditioning-improvement claim because boundary-front mediation weakens and the conditioned-vs-null pass condition fails.

## 4. External Resemblance
- The current structure resembles a typed interface or role ledger more than a substance ontology.
- The word `condition` behaves like a governed role name, not like a self-standing thing, in the documents reviewed here.

## 5. What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove the retest is exhaustive across all possible conditioning variants.
