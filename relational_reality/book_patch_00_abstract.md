### Scope Guardrails and Reader Orientation
The framework described in this book is a mathematical and relational scaffolding. It is intended as a strict, internally consistent analog layer—a way to structurally map and understand the generative processes that lead to stable phenomena. The framework is exploratory and analogical in scope.

**Claim Boundary:**
This framework does not claim to replace established physics, nor does it present an alternative to the Standard Model or General Relativity. No empirical replacement claim is asserted. It offers a procedural analog framework for interpreting persistence, relation, and projection.

Throughout this text, any terminology that intersects with classical or modern physics—such as $space\_app$, $time\_app$, $gravity\_app$, or $field\_app$—is explicitly marked with an analog suffix. This $physics\_app$ terminology denotes projection-layer interpretation only. The reader is encouraged to engage with the equations and operators not as literal laws of the universe, but as an operational grammar for how distinction and constraint can generate recognizable structure.
