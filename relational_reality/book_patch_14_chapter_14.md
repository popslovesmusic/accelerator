### 14.5 Distributed Stabilization and Persistence Enhancement

When cooperation holds, interacting organizations can produce **distributed stabilization**: stabilization that persists across the coupled system even when local perturbations occur. This is expressed as cross-conditioned residue reinforcement across the interaction boundary:
$$\sum R_{shared} \rightarrow Stability_{distributed}$$
$R_{shared}$ denotes residue contributions that remain mutually coherent under coupling (shared in the sense of cross-conditioned persistence, not shared as a centralized store). $Stability_{distributed}$ denotes the persistence benefit that emerges when many local routing interactions reinforce re-enterability rather than fragment it.

Within this framework, distributed stabilization denotes persistence enhancement arising from mutually reinforcing admissibility interactions distributed across coupled routing organizations.

Distributed stabilization is not necessarily global. It can be local and domain-dependent:
- local cooperative pockets may remain stable within a larger destabilizing environment,

In the projection-layer ethical interpretation, “ethical organization” is the organizational regime in which distributed stabilization is enhanced by interaction structures that preserve mutual residue coherence.

Cooperative stabilization does not imply permanent equilibrium or universal compatibility across all admissibility regimes.
