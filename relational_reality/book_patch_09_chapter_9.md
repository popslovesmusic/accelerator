# Chapter 9: Cosmos\_app, Time\_app, and Near-Empty Structure

### 9.0 Formal Anchor: $\prec_{global} \rightarrow time_{app}$

Chapter 8 introduced accumulation\_regime\_app and geometry\_app as projection-layer appearances of globally stabilized admissibility shaping. This chapter continues that derivation into domain-scale organization. When non-commutative ordering and reinforcement persist across large regions, the resulting global ordering density projects as time\_app, and the globally organized continuation domain projects as cosmos\_app.

We maintain strict projection discipline: time\_app and cosmos\_app are not treated as primitive background structures. They are projection-layer consequences of stabilized continuation organization under admissibility constraints.

---

### 9.1 Global Ordering and Continuation Density

Local corridor reinforcement produces $G_{shape}$ as a coarse-grained global shaping relation (Chapter 8). At larger scale, many such shaping relations coexist and interact across the substrate. The result is not a single corridor or a single basin, but a domain-scale organization of reachability, arbitration preference, and residue-conditioned admissibility.

We refer to the density and persistence of this organization as **global ordering density**: the degree to which admissible continuation repeatedly selects consistent ordering relations across broad portions of the orientation array. Global ordering density is therefore not a material density; it is a measure of how strongly ordered continuation persists and repeats under $A$, $R$, and $I$ constraints.

This prepares the temporal projection: time\_app is derived from stabilized ordering asymmetry, not assumed as a universal flow parameter.

### 9.2 The Derivation of Time\_app

The formal basis for time\_app is the non-commutativity of ordered updates. From the Arrow of Ordering:
$$\Phi_{t+1}\circ\Phi_t \neq \Phi_t\circ\Phi_{t+1}$$
When residue updates are non-commutative, ordering becomes path-dependent, and continuation cannot be reduced to a reversible sequence without loss. At sufficient stabilization, this ordering asymmetry persists across many local cycles and becomes a domain-level stabilized ordering relation within continuation organization.

We name the projection consequence of this stabilized global ordering as:
$$\prec_{global} \rightarrow time_{app}$$
Here, $\prec_{global}$ denotes the large-scale persistence of procedural ordering relations. time\_app denotes the projection-layer appearance of this stabilized ordering as a temporal signature.

This derivation rejects primitive universal time assumptions. It does not claim that "time does not exist" or that temporal experience is illusory. It only asserts that, within this framework, time\_app is a projection consequence of stabilized non-commutative continuation ordering.

### 9.3 Projection-Layer Temporality

Procedural ordering ($\prec$) is the primitive. Clock-like time is a projection-layer instrument: a structured summarization of ordering density and ordering asymmetry into a compressed operational projection representation for comparative ordering analysis.

Because ordering is local and residue-conditioned, projection-layer temporality can vary by domain: different regions can exhibit different stabilization depths, different reinforcement persistence, and different ordering-density signatures. time\_app therefore denotes a family of projection-layer temporal appearances governed by the same non-commutative ordering constraint, not a single metaphysical flow.

### 9.4 Cosmos\_app as Global Continuation Domain

At large scale, the accumulated shaping relations ($G_{shape}$) across many regions define the overall organization of what continuation can access, sustain, and traverse under admissibility constraints. We introduce cosmos\_app as the projection-layer appearance of this globally organized continuation domain.

We represent the coarse-grained derivation as:
$$\sum G_{shape} \rightarrow cosmos_{app}$$
cosmos\_app denotes the large-scale projection appearance of globally organized continuation domains within the framework and does not assert a literal cosmological substrate model. This organizational structure is relational rather than spatially substantive and should not be interpreted as a literal cosmological substrate. No cosmological replacement claim is made. The intent is organizational: cosmos\_app summarizes global reachability organization and large-scale admissibility structure.

### 9.5 Near-Empty Structure

A common interpretive error is to treat the absence of stabilized basins as literal emptiness. Within this framework, absolute emptiness is not admitted as a persistent regime, because continuation is governed by the generative coupling:
$$(\mathcal{E}\neq0)\Leftrightarrow_R\delta_a(\mathcal{E}>0)$$
Non-null distinguishability and admissible continuation are recursively coupled under residue conditioning. The framework therefore treats complete null-state persistence as structurally unstable.

We define **near-empty structure** as **minimal admissibility organization**: a regime of low corridor density, sparse reinforcement persistence, and weakly stabilized reachability gradients. Near-empty structure is not a void. It is a low-density organization of admissible continuation in which reinforcement is insufficient to generate strong accumulation\_regime\_app or a robust geometry\_app appearance, but in which residual continuation persists.

### 9.6 Minimal Distinguishability and Residual Continuation

Near-empty structure retains a distinguishability floor: mismatch can be minimal, but cannot be driven to a permanently null condition without violating the generative coupling. Within this framework, the distinguishability floor denotes the minimum non-null mismatch persistence admissible under recursive continuation coupling. In practice, this means:

- residual continuation candidates still exist (preferred terminology: "residual continuation candidates" for low-density continuation persistence states),
- admissibility windows still gate transitions,
- residue still conditions subsequent ordering, even if weakly.

We keep the scope restrained: this chapter does not promote a physicalized "vacuum theory". It establishes only the procedural requirement that near-empty structure remains non-null and that minimal admissibility organization can persist as a low-reinforcement regime.

This provides a cautious bridge toward later quantum-scale discussion topics, but does not claim to explain external quantum phenomena.

### 9.7 Expansion\_app and Organizational Relaxation

We introduce expansion\_app only as a projection-layer analogy: large-scale admissibility redistribution and organizational relaxation. When global ordering density and reinforcement are not uniform, continuation organization can reorganize over time\_app signatures, producing a changing global reachability structure.

Expansion\_app does not imply literal metric expansion of a primitive substrate and remains a projection-layer organizational analogy only. Redistribution within this framework refers to changing admissibility preference organization rather than motion through an expanding container-space. In this framing, expansion\_app does not replace cosmological expansion theory and does not claim to explain dark energy. It denotes the projection appearance of large-scale admissibility redistribution within these models.

### 9.8 Transition to Ratchets and Asymmetric Continuation

Global organization constrains what local continuation can exploit. When ordering asymmetry and reinforcement persistence exist, local systems can harness these constraints as an engineering resource: they can bias continuation selection, stabilize directional cycles, and amplify small asymmetries into persistent outcomes.

The next chapter returns to local mechanisms—ratchets and rowing—as applied forms of asymmetric continuation. We treat local asymmetry exploitation as a procedural engineering consequence of organized continuation structure, not as a claim about external thermodynamic law replacement.
