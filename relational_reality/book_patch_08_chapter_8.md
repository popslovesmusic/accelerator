# Chapter 8: Accumulation, Geometry\_app, and Large-Scale Admissibility Shaping

### 8.0 Formal Anchor: $\sum C_{corridor} \rightarrow G_{shape}$

Chapter 7 derived transport\_regime\_app as ordered continuation propagation along reinforced continuation corridors ($C_{corridor}$). This chapter extends that derivation across scale: when corridor reinforcement becomes dense and persistent across the substrate, local accessibility preferences aggregate into a global shaping of admissibility. The resulting organization projects, at the analogy layer, as geometry\_app.

We use **accumulation\_regime\_app** to name the regime in which corridor density and residue-conditioned reinforcement no longer behave as isolated pathways, but as a coherent large-scale constraint structure on reachability. Geometry\_app is treated here as a **projection-layer appearance** of that constraint organization, not as a primitive container-space substrate.

---

### 8.1 From Corridors to Global Organization

A single continuation corridor is a local history-conditioned accessibility preference. However, the substrate is not limited to one corridor at a time: multiple pressure sources generate multiple reinforced sequences. As these sequences persist, corridor density increases, and corridor intersections and overlaps become common.

At sufficient density, reinforcement is no longer adequately described as a set of discrete pathways. The net effect is a global biasing of reachability: for many loci, the admissibility window $A$ is conditioned not only by immediate local residue, but by the aggregated corridor structure that repeatedly routes continuation through a constrained subset of the orientation array.

We formalize this transition by introducing a coarse-grained global shaping relation:
$$\sum C_{corridor} \rightarrow G_{shape}$$
$G_{shape}$ denotes the large-scale admissibility shaping induced by the accumulated reinforcement of corridors. It is not an additional substance; it is the coarse-grained organization of what continuation can access under residue- and orientation-conditioned admissibility.
Within this framework, coarse-graining denotes a projection-layer compression of large-scale continuation organization rather than a literal averaging of geometric substance.

### 8.2 Accumulation\_regime\_app

**accumulation\_regime\_app** names the projection-layer appearance of stabilized large-scale admissibility shaping. Accumulation is not material stacking or storage. It is the persistence of reinforcement structure across many cycles, such that:

1. corridor reinforcement survives perturbation,
2. overlap does not erase coherence, and
3. reachability becomes globally biased.

In this regime, mismatch pressure propagation ($P_\Delta$) no longer expresses primarily as local transport along isolated corridors. Instead, it contributes to a sustained shaping of admissible continuation capacity across the substrate:
$$P_\Delta \rightarrow G_{shape}$$
This is a derivational continuity statement: unresolved pressure, repeatedly arbitrated through reinforced corridors, produces a stable global shaping of admissibility.

### 8.3 The Emergence of Geometry\_app

Geometry\_app is introduced as the projection-layer appearance of $G_{shape}$. The key discipline is to keep the mapping direction explicit:

- the substrate evolves procedurally through admissible continuation and residue,
- $G_{shape}$ summarizes accumulated constraint organization at scale,
- geometry\_app is a coarse-grained appearance of that organization.

We retain the Ordering Law as the primitive definition of accessibility:
$$x \prec x' \iff x' \in A(x;r,K) \text{ and } x' \text{ is reachable via } I$$
On this basis, geometry\_app is not defined by pre-existing distances. Instead, apparent distance-like structure is a projection of reachability under $G_{shape}$. Two loci appear "near" when they are mutually reachable through reinforced admissibility channels; they appear "far" when reachability requires high residue-conditioned arbitration cost or is repeatedly rejected by $A$.

Within the framework, the commonly observed three-dimensionality of spatial appearance is treated as a projection consequence of triadic stabilization:
$$\mathrm{rank}(C_K)=3 \implies \mathrm{dim}_{app}=3$$
This does not assert that the substrate is literally embedded in a 3D container; it records that the stable triadic closure regime supplies a minimal basis that projects as three dominant continuation dimensions at the appearance layer.

### 8.4 Geodesic-like Continuation

Once $G_{shape}$ stabilizes, continuation is not equally likely across all admissible candidates. The process exhibits **geodesic-like continuation**: preferred continuation sequences that follow the strongest stabilized reachability gradients induced by accumulated reinforcement.

This is not introduced as a replacement for general relativity. It is a procedural statement: under large-scale admissibility shaping, the arbitration of continuation candidates tends to select routes that remain inside the most coherent reinforcement structure, because those routes maximize admissible continuation under residue-conditioned constraints.

Geodesic-like behavior therefore refers to **continuation preference under $G_{shape}$**, not to motion of objects through a pre-existing spacetime.

### 8.5 Mass\_app and Corridor Density

We introduce **mass\_app** cautiously as an analogy-layer interpretation of **organizational persistence density**. In accumulation\_regime\_app, regions of high corridor density are regions where reinforcement structure is deep, persistent, and strongly biasing.

We can describe this as a persistence burden: maintaining non-aligned continuation through a high-density reinforcement region requires repeated arbitration against the prevailing admissibility shaping. Within this framework, persistence burden denotes the arbitration cost associated with continuation against dominant admissibility shaping. The projection-layer appearance of this persistence burden can be described as mass\_app.

mass\_app therefore refers to organized persistence-density effects at the projection layer rather than intrinsic material substance.

Mass\_app is not treated as an intrinsic substance property, and this chapter does not claim to replace particle-mass theories. It is a descriptive projection of corridor reinforcement density and the corresponding narrowing of continuation flexibility.

### 8.6 Gravity\_regime\_app as Accumulation Projection

Following the cautious scope of TN_Gravity_Refinement_v05, we introduce **gravity\_regime\_app** as a projection-layer interpretation of large-scale admissibility shaping. In this framing, gravity\_regime\_app is:

- analogous to large-scale admissibility shaping,
- a projection-layer interpretation of accumulated reinforcement,
- a resemblance to geodesic-like continuation organization.

gravity\_regime\_app is introduced solely as an organizational analogy-layer interpretation of large-scale admissibility shaping and should not be interpreted as a derivational replacement for general relativity.

It is not asserted to be gravity, and no replacement claim is made for general relativity. The statement here is structural: when $G_{shape}$ is non-uniform across the substrate, continuation preferences become biased in a way that can resemble ordered continuation deflection at the appearance layer.

### 8.7 Curvature Without Substance

To prevent ontology leakage, we explicitly reject the interpretation that geometry\_app implies a literal geometric substance or a primitive spacetime fabric. Curvature\_app, when used, refers to variation in $G_{shape}$—variation in the organization of reachability and arbitration across the orientation array—rather than deformation of a material fabric.

In this sense:

- there is no transport medium,
- there is no container-space requirement,
- geometry\_app is an appearance of organized reachability.

### 8.8 Transition to Cosmos\_app and Time\_app

The next chapters require a bridge from local regimes to global organization. Accumulation\_regime\_app provides that bridge: as $G_{shape}$ stabilizes over very large scales, ordering and accessibility patterns become domain-level invariants.

This sets up two additional projection-layer topics:

1. **cosmos\_app:** the appearance of a globally organized continuation domain under large-scale admissibility shaping.
2. **time\_app:** the appearance of directed temporal structure as a projection of stabilized non-commutative ordering ($\prec$) under persistent global reinforcement.

time\_app therefore emerges as a projection consequence of stabilized non-commutative continuation ordering rather than as a primitive universal flow parameter.

cosmos\_app denotes the large-scale projection appearance of globally organized continuation domains within the framework and does not assert a literal cosmological substrate model.

We will treat these as projection consequences of accumulated continuation organization and ordering-density effects, not as primitive background structures.
