# Formal Non-Completeness Boundary

## Status

`BOUNDARY_DECLARED_NOT_PROOF` — C1.

## Authorized conclusion

The current evidence is insufficient to claim that the calculus is complete, sound for all models, consistent, or model-independent.

## Basis

Only two finite relational models and a minimal conjunction rule set were tested. The model comparison showed that admissibility, closure, and relation truth varied by model. The general grammar, semantic interpretation, partial-operator semantics, and broad inference system remain incomplete. No completeness or consistency proof was produced.

## Boundary distinction

This is a formal claim boundary for the current evidence. It is not a proof that no complete calculus can be constructed.

## Release conditions

The boundary may be reconsidered only after broader model-class semantics, a complete typed grammar, general inference soundness, a completeness or impossibility result, and independent review are available.

No registry or textbook promotion is authorized by this boundary.
