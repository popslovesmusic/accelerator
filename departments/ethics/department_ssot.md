# Ethics Department Single Source of Truth (SSOT)

This document is the local SSOT for the Ethics Department (`ethics_app`).

It governs ethical-domain interpretation, derived vocabulary, provisional models, evaluation proposals, and validation status for ethics-facing work derived from the Mono-Process Framework.

The Ethics Department is subordinate to the global core and the Mathematics Department. It does not define RT/Core, primitive operators, formal admissibility, formal residue, or theorem status. It interprets selected formal structures as possible ethical-domain projections through explicit derivation and correspondence rules.

---

## Department Charter

The Ethics Department exists to investigate what organizational structures emerge when the primitive ontology is projected into the ethical domain.

Boundary rule:

Mathematics defines the relational calculus. Ethics interprets selected formal structures as possible descriptions of ethical organization, standing, projection, and evaluation through explicit derivation and correspondence rules.

Methodological rule:

No ethical primitive may be introduced unless it is derivable from the core ontology.

---

## Scope and Boundaries

### In Scope
- ethical interpretation,
- derived moral vocabulary,
- process-based standing and organization models,
- admissibility-facing ethical correspondences,
- evaluation proposals,
- comparison to legacy ethical vocabularies,
- validation and falsification status for ethics-facing models.

### Out of Scope
- redefining RT/Core,
- redefining `<*>_x`,
- redefining `D(a|b)`,
- redefining admissibility as a formal operator condition,
- redefining residue as a formal trace condition,
- introducing `good` or `bad` as primitives,
- promoting classical state-based ethical properties into the ontology without reduction,
- promoting local ethical interpretations into global ontology.

### Claim Ceiling
Unless separately validated, Ethics Department outputs are capped at:
- `C1_model_relative` for derivation scaffolds,
- `C3_structural_comparison` for ethical-domain interpretations and comparisons,
- `C2_simulation_observed` only when a specific governed ethics-facing model has recoverable evidence.

No ethics-facing statement may treat a projected ethical category as primitive, or present a local ethical interpretation as a universal proof.

## Prohibited Promotions

The following are blocked:
- introducing `good` or `bad` as primitives,
- treating state-based ethical categories as ontological foundations without reduction,
- promoting local ethical interpretation to universal truth,
- treating ethics as a replacement foundation for the core ontology,
- claiming theorem status for ethical derivations without formal support,
- importing independent moral axioms that are not licensed by the core ontology.
