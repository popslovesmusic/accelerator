# Physics Department Single Source of Truth (SSOT)

This document is the local SSOT for the Physics Department.

It governs physical interpretation, correspondence rules, provisional models, predictions, and validation status for physics-facing work derived from the Mono-Process Framework.

The Physics Department is subordinate to the global core and the Mathematics Department. It does not define RT/Core, primitive operators, formal admissibility, formal residue, or theorem status. In this SSOT, every primitive is treated as RT-governed and traced to `RT_core`, but not every RT-governed expression is primitive; RT-derived continuations, projections, and operational regimes remain RT without being primitive. It interprets selected formal structures as possible descriptions of observable nature through explicit correspondence rules.

---

## Department Charter

The Physics Department exists to interpret governed formal structures as possible physical models without changing the underlying ontology or promoting formal consistency to empirical confirmation.

Boundary rule:

Mathematics defines the relational calculus. Physics interprets selected formal structures as possible descriptions of observable nature through explicit correspondence rules.

---

## Scope and Boundaries

### In Scope
- physical interpretation,
- model correspondence,
- observable mapping,
- prediction proposals,
- comparison to known physics,
- validation and falsification status,
- provisional analog models.

### Out of Scope
- redefining RT/Core,
- redefining `<*>_x`,
- redefining `D(a|b)`,
- redefining admissibility as a formal operator condition,
- redefining residue as a formal trace condition,
- redefining closure,
- redefining conditioning reduction,
- promoting formal lemmas or theorems locally,
- promoting speculative physics into global ontology.

### Claim Ceiling
Unless separately validated, Physics Department outputs are capped at:
- `C1_model_relative` for correspondence scaffolds,
- `C3_structural_comparison` for analog or interpretation claims,
- `C2_simulation_observed` only when a specific governed physics-facing model has recoverable evidence.

No physics-facing statement may treat analogy as identity or formal derivation as empirical proof.

## Prohibited Promotions

The following are blocked:
- formal derivation to physics claim without correspondence rule,
- physics interpretation to empirical result without evidence,
- analogy to identity,
- successful model to universal truth,
- physical terminology as a backdoor for new primitives,
- any claim that the framework has proven external physical reality from internal formal closure alone.
