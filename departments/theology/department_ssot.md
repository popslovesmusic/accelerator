# Theology Department Single Source of Truth (SSOT)

This document is the local SSOT for the Theology Department (`theology_app` / `procedural_theology_app`).

It governs theology-domain interpretation, derived vocabulary, provisional comparison rules, validation status, and department-local claim boundaries for theology-facing work derived from the Mono-Process Framework.

The Theology Department is subordinate to the global core and the Mathematics Department. It does not define RT/Core, primitive operators, formal admissibility, formal residue, or theorem status. In this SSOT, every primitive is treated as RT-governed and traced to `RT_core`, but not every RT-governed expression is primitive; RT-derived continuations, projections, and operational regimes remain RT without being primitive. It interprets selected formal structures as theology-domain projections through explicit derivation, correspondence, and analogy rules.

The detailed theology domain registry remains in `docs/theology/procedural_theology_ssot.md`. This department SSOT is the peer-department governance surface for work under `departments/theology/`.
When theology work consults live governance or patch-chain state, request an explicit evidence level from `scripts/query_governance.py`; summary is the default for routine checks, diagnostic and governance modes are for review, and forensic mode is reserved for full reconstruction.

---

## Department Charter

The Theology Department exists to study whether theological vocabularies can be expressed as governed semantic projections of the same process ontology already used elsewhere in the framework.

Boundary rule:

Mathematics defines the relational calculus. Theology interprets selected formal structures as possible theology-domain projections through explicit derivation, correspondence, and analogy rules.

Methodological rule:

No theological primitive may be introduced by vocabulary substitution, and no structural resemblance may be promoted to doctrinal identity or external proof.

## Scope and Boundaries

### In Scope
- theology-domain interpretation,
- derived theological vocabulary,
- structural comparison and governed analogy,
- local admissibility rules for theology-facing language,
- validation and falsification status for theology-facing mappings,
- bounded cross-domain `proof_app` comparison.

### Out of Scope
- redefining RT/Core,
- redefining `<*>_x`,
- redefining `D(a|b)`,
- redefining admissibility as a formal operator condition,
- redefining residue as a formal trace condition,
- claiming RT proves theology,
- claiming theology is foundational to mathematics,
- promoting doctrinal identity from structural resemblance,
- promoting theology-domain projections into global ontology.

### Claim Ceiling
Unless separately validated, Theology Department outputs are capped at:
- `C0_definition` for local vocabulary declarations,
- `C1_model_relative` for internal formal mappings,
- `C3_structural_comparison` for external theological resemblance claims.

No theology-facing statement may be promoted to doctrine proof, historical proof, or unrestricted metaphysical confirmation.
