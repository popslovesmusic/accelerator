## Sphere of Influence and coupling



## Computation and reference alignment

Computation is not performed by an isolated RT. The computational surface becomes active only under lawful coupling. Reference alignment is the proposed bridge between coupling and computation:


The proposal separates interaction and continuation:

- coupling is an inter-RT boundary phenomenon;
- propagation is an intra-RT continuation phenomenon.
