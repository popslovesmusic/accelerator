### Suggested Research Questions for Falsification

**Question 1: Symmetry Collapse under Extreme Coupling**
*   **Question:** Does the `NOT_axiom` (the prohibition of $\epsilon = 0$) hold in regimes of extreme `coupling` ($K$) and `density` ($\rho$), or can a system be driven into a state of "Perfect Symmetry" (total decoupling)?
*   **Relation to the Whole:** This question targets the fundamental premise (Section I/L0) that perfect symmetry is inadmissible. If a simulation demonstrates a stable, zero-mismatch state under high-pressure conditions, the core axiom of the "One Process"—that difference is a structural necessity—is falsified.

**Question 2: Admissibility Dead-Ends and Process Termination**
*   **Question:** Can a specific configuration of accumulated `Residue` ($R$) and `Causal Sphere of Influence` (CSI) generate an `Admissibility` set ($\mathcal{A}$) that is empty, effectively forcing the process to stop?
*   **Relation to the Whole:** This challenges the `Continuation Law` (Section III/L1), which asserts that an admissible next state must always exist. If the "filter" of admissibility (Section V/L3) can be shown to block all possible futures, the theory's claim of universal persistence is invalidated.

**Question 3: Residue-Free Identity (Stabilization without Memory)**
*   **Question:** Is it possible to maintain stable `Identity` (L9) or a `cognition_SRC` purely through `Orientation` alignment ($-(i)$) without the functional presence or accumulation of `Residue` ($R$)?
*   **Relation to the Whole:** This tests the claim in Section IV.2/L2 that `Residue` is *necessary* to "sustain the constraint internally" and carry structure. If identity can stabilize as a "ghost" pattern without historical trace-writing, the theory's reliance on residue as the substrate of reality is falsified.
