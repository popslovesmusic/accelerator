## 5. Distinction: Relational Asymmetry vs. Metric Asymmetry
It is critical to distinguish between the generative *domain condition* and the observational *metric*:

- **Relational Asymmetry (`(asym_app)nDOF`)**: A generative domain condition where directed distinctions $D(S1|S2) \ne D(S2|S1)$ induce an orientation preference $-(i)$. This is the primary generator of orientation.
- **Metric Asymmetry ($\Omega_a = D(S1|S2)/D(S2|S1)$)**: A scalar observational metric used to quantify the degree of asymmetry *after* the domain condition has induced an orientation.
- **Projections**: The domain structure is mapped to scalar metrics via the projection operator $\Leftrightarrow_m$ and to statistical realizations via $\Leftrightarrow_s$.

This distinction clarifies that $\Omega_a$ is an observational tool, not the generative source of orientation.
