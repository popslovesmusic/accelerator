$$ (\mathcal{E} \neq 0) \iff_R \delta_a(\mathcal{E} > 0) $$

$$ U_{\Omega} := [ \chi_D \xrightarrow{\mathcal{E}} \delta_a \xrightarrow{\text{Arb}_A} \Delta \xrightarrow{} \chi_D' ] $$

## 3. Residue and History-Conditioned Admissibility

A key differentiator of the Mono-Process Framework is that its "laws" are not external, eternal edicts. Admissibility is conditioned by **Residue ($R$)**.

Therefore, the legality landscape of future events is itself a process-generated structure. Persistence is achieved not through static equality, but through *residue-admissible identity continuation*, where a localized process maintains its relational signature over sequential updates.
