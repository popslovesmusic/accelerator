That mutual conditioning isn't free — it runs through a filter. Raw difference only becomes persistent difference if it falls within an admissibility window. Each cycle is not a reset — it is an accumulation.

From that basic cycle, interaction between processes is inherently asymmetric. How A registers B is not the same as how B registers A. That order-dependence is not a quirk — it is structurally necessary. Symmetric interaction produces no gradient, no pressure, no direction. It is one of the collapse modes. What persists must be asymmetric.

That asymmetry accumulates. The directional surplus between how A registers B and how B registers A generates a primitive continuation tension — recursive phase mismatch pressure. This is not force, not energy, not metric curvature. It is the tension that must be resolved by some organizational regime or else drive the system toward transition, decay, or reconfiguration. Everything that follows is the story of how that pressure gets resolved.

Where three or more processes lock into mutually reinforcing asymmetry with shared residue-history and compatible orientation, something stable forms. That last point is the key. Mismatch pressure can only be resolved in one of four ways, depending on what organizational structure it encounters. These four resolutions are what look like forces from inside the projection.

The first resolution is transformation. Where only two processes interact, there is not enough recursive depth to achieve closure. The two-node system cannot generate the reinforcement circulation that would let it hold its mismatch internally. The pressure has nowhere to go but out — so the system changes. It reconfigures its internal coupling structure, sheds the excess, becomes something else. This is not a failure. It is structurally expected and structurally productive. Decay events in this regime are admissibility-threshold crossings that redirect mismatch pressure into new continuation organizations. What looks like a particle changing type and emitting something is directional pressure finding the only continuation pathway still admissible. This is the weak organizational projection regime: pressure discharge through transformation, operating below the triadic closure threshold.

The second resolution is confinement. Where three processes achieve triadic closure, the interior mismatch volume operates at maximal occupancy pressure. The coupling is such that separating any pair requires work against the full interior circulation — dispersal of one node increases mismatch pressure across the remaining pair, driving a restoring continuation pressure that resists separation. The system holds its pressure internally through reinforcement. The coupling that maintains interior coherence is not an exchanged particle — it is the ongoing arbitration process sustaining the triadic interior. Confinement emerges because separation would require crossing an admissibility boundary beyond which the interior circulation cannot sustain itself. The coherence cavity fails and the released pressure immediately reorganizes into new triadic closures rather than permit indefinite separation. This is the strong organizational projection regime: pressure internalization through triadic confinement, operating at maximum occupancy saturation.

The third resolution is propagation. Between transformation and confinement there is a third organizational threshold — one that achieves neither. A persistent transport channel sustains phase coherence across distance without closing into a basin and without decaying into transformation. The channel moves its mismatch rather than holding it or shedding it. It is reinforced by the passage of mismatch itself — each propagation event strengthens the pathway for the next, creating a self-sustaining directional corridor. This is not a wave in a medium. It is resonant continuation transport in a self-reinforcing pathway. Unlike confinement, the transport channel leaves the surrounding arbitration topology relatively unaffected — it establishes a local directional preference structure along its pathway without globally deforming the candidate landscape. A stronger nearby channel can redirect transport, producing the analog of screening. This is the electromotive organizational projection regime: pressure propagation through directional transport channels, subject to competition and screening.

The fourth resolution is accumulation. Every stabilized triadic closure contributes a recursive mismatch volume to the substrate. That volume generates admissibility occupancy pressure that propagates outward, attenuating with distance but never vanishing within finite accessibility range. As coherent basins stabilize, reinforcement pathways intensify, admissibility margins narrow, and continuation freedom decreases in their vicinity. Neighboring loci experience reduced traversal diversity — the range of available continuation candidates narrows. This occupancy pressure accumulates across the entire orientation array. The routes through that accumulated preference structure that candidates consistently favor are what look like geodesics. The rate at which that preference structure changes across neighboring loci is what looks like curvature. Objects do not fall toward mass. Their continuation candidates are progressively arbitrated toward basin-reinforced trajectories through recursively overlapping mismatch volumes and admissibility occupancy gradients — until non-aligned paths become too costly to sustain. This is the gravity organizational projection regime: unbounded accumulation of occupancy pressure deforming the global arbitration topology. Unlike the other three regimes, gravity has no saturation mechanism. Strong force saturates at the triadic interior. Electromagnetic transport is channeled and subject to screening. Weak transformation is self-terminating. Gravity accumulates without ceiling because it operates through volume overlap rather than interior pressure — and because there is no screening mechanism, contributions sum across every volume within the accessibility domain.

Gravity is weak because it is late.
