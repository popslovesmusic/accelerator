# Procedural Theology Single Source of Truth (SSOT)

This document is the authoritative local SSOT for the Theology Department (`procedural_theology_app`).

The department is established as a semantic projection layer under global Mono-Process governance. It does not function as a source of primitive ontology, external doctrinal proof, or unrestricted metaphysical promotion.

---

## Department Charter & Scope

### Scope & Mission
The Theology Department (`procedural_theology_app`) is established to study whether theological vocabularies can be expressed as governed semantic projections of the same process ontology already used elsewhere in the framework.

The department inherits the same RT/Core and process-first ontology as every other department:
- Process is primitive.
- Distinction is non-null tension internal to continuing process.
- Relational coupling is constitutive rather than decorative.
- History is carried through residue rather than imported as an external timeline.

The department therefore treats theological nouns as candidate semantic projections, interpretive labels, or analogical mappings unless and until a narrower governed role is explicitly defined.

### Core Governance Position
The department does **not** claim:
- that RT proves any theology,
- that theology is foundational to the mathematics,
- that doctrinal identity follows from structural resemblance,
- that analogy may be promoted to metaphysical or historical fact.

The department **does** permit:
- structural comparison,
- semantic instantiation,
- governed analogy,
- cross-domain `proof_app` comparison at the level of practical formal invariance.

### Department Definition
**Procedural Theology** is the governed study of theological interpretations as semantic projections of RT/Core under department-local vocabulary, domain-local evidence standards, and explicit analogy/identity separation.

## 3. Theology-Specific Guardrails

### 3.1 Analogy Lock
The following readings are prohibited unless separately justified with explicit governance approval:
- `RT = theology`
- `RT proves Trinity`
- `RT proves Incarnation`
- `RT proves God`
- `formal resemblance = doctrinal identity`

### 3.2 Allowed Comparative Language
Preferred language includes:
- "structurally resembles"
- "admits a theology-domain interpretation"
- "can be read as a governed semantic projection"
- "does not establish doctrinal identity"
- "does not prove external theological truth"

### 3.3 Evidence Handling
Theology-domain work must distinguish:
1. internal framework definitions,
2. cross-domain structural comparisons,
3. historical or doctrinal claims not licensed by framework structure alone.
