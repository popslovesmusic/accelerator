# Research Project: The Residue Hysteresis Hypothesis
## Hypothesis: Internal Sustainability of the NOT Axiom

### 1. Theoretical Grounding
Based on Section IV.2 of "THE LAW OF THE ONE PROCESS":
> "Residue is required to sustain the constraint internally... without residue, the system would require external enforcement of ε ≠ 0."

### 2. Formal Hypothesis
**The Residue Hysteresis Hypothesis:**
Accumulated `residue` ($R$) functions as an autonomous structural constraint. A system that has established high levels of $R$ will maintain non-zero `mismatch` ($\epsilon$) even when external `coupling` ($K$) is reduced to zero. This demonstrates that the process is "internally sustained" by its own history, fulfilling the `NOT_axiom` without external pressure.

### 3. Canonical Mapping
- **Mismatch (ε):** Quantified as `order_parameter` or `active_fraction`.
- **Residue (R):** Quantified as `residue_mean`.
- **Coupling (K):** Quantified as `K_phi` (interaction strength).

### 4. Experimental Strategy (Multi-Model)

**Phase 1: Primary Simulation (Agent-Based)**
- **Tool:** `agent_based_sim_v1`
- **Objective:** Induction of $R$.
- **Method:** Drive the system with $K=0.8$ to accumulate high $R$, then use `bifurcation_analyzer_v1` to ramp $K \to 0$.
- **Prediction:** The `order_parameter` will show a "hysteresis loop," remaining non-zero longer than a control run with zero initial $R$.

**Phase 2: Secondary Simulation (Cellular Automata)**
- **Tool:** `ca_admissibility_sim_v1`
- **Objective:** Cross-verify "Zero Logic" in a discrete field.
- **Method:** Test the `residue_growth` vs `residue_decay` relationship.

**Phase 3: Analysis & Verification**
- **Analysis Layer:** `info_metrics_module_v1` (to correlate $R$ with system complexity) and `tda_module_v1` (to verify the topology of the persistent mismatch).
- **Falsification:** If $\epsilon \to 0$ simultaneously with $K \to 0$ despite high $R$, the hypothesis is falsified.
