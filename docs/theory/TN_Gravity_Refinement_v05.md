**1. Foundational Position**

Within the Mono-Process Framework, gravity_app is not treated as force,
attraction, primitive geometry, or independent field structure. It is a
higher-order projection emerging from ordered recursive continuation
operating within a continuous deformable substrate.

The substrate is continuous, recursively coupled, mismatch-mediated,
orientation-sensitive, and governed by admissibility-constrained
continuation. Persistent structures are not entities placed inside space
--- they are coherent recursive deformations of the substrate itself.

This note presents the complete derivation: from ordered relational
asymmetry through recursive mismatch volumes, admissibility occupancy
pressure, and arbitration topology, to gravity_app. It additionally
formalizes the vacuum fluctuation analog --- the structural account of
why new persistent structures can emerge from the minimum-gradient
regime --- which the framework requires but prior versions left
implicit.

**2. Ordered Relational Asymmetry as Foundational Engine**

The minimum realizable persistent structure is a recursively coupled
triadic closure regime requiring maintained non-zero admissible
detectable mismatch:

D(S_i \| S_j) \> 0

Relational ordering is fundamentally non-equivalent --- not measurement
error or perspective inversion, but primitive procedural asymmetry:

D(S1\|S2) \> 0 ≠ D(S2\|S1) \> 0

Realized mismatch is the operational directional imbalance:

Δ_R = D(S1\|S2)\>0 − D(S2\|S1)\>0

Δ_R is not scalar mismatch magnitude. It is the directional surplus that
drives orientation, arbitration bias, continuation preference, recursive
basin stabilization, and emergent curvature tendency. When Δ_R → 0,
arbitration landscapes flatten, orientation pressure weakens, and the
system trends toward reversible symmetry. Non-zero Δ_R produces
admissibility anisotropy --- the condition under which curvature_app can
exist at all.

Curvature_app is therefore not generated from mismatch magnitude alone,
but from ordered mismatch asymmetry recursively propagated through
continuation structure.

**3. Recursive Mismatch Volumes**

Triadic structures are not edge-defined geometric enclosures. Because
the substrate is continuous and mismatch-mediated, the interior of every
triadic closure regime participates in recursive continuation dynamics.
The triad is a recursive mismatch volume.

The interior contains recursive admissibility structure, continuation
arbitration, propagation redistribution, orientation gradients, and
active mismatch circulation. The triadic peaks act as reinforcement
maxima and orientation anchors. The interior is not passive --- it is
the active regime where continuation candidates are generated,
recursively arbitrated, and selectively reinforced.

**3.1 Volume Overlap and Coherence Cavity Formation**

When multiple recursive mismatch volumes couple, their interiors
overlap. The overlap produces coherence cavities: shared arbitration
regimes, reinforcement interference patterns, and distributed
admissibility basins B_U (LAW-011). Geometry_app (LAW-010) emerges from
this overlap structure, not from edge topology alone.

**3.2 The No-Void Consequence and Vacuum_app**

There are no true voids. Only varying mismatch density, continuation
pressure, arbitration intensity, and admissibility gradient structure.
What is conventionally described as empty space is a minimum-gradient
continuation regime --- a region where triadic volumes are sparse,
coherence cavities are weakly reinforced, and G_A(U) (LAW-031)
approaches its structural floor rather than zero.

The gradient cannot reach zero. The NOT-Axiom (ε ≠ 0) prohibits complete
symmetry collapse: the substrate maintains background mismatch
circulation even in the total absence of localized persistent
structures. Vacuum_app is therefore minimum-gradient process, not
ontological emptiness.

**4. Vacuum Fluctuation Analog**

The no-void consequence has a structural corollary that the framework
requires but prior versions left implicit: the vacuum_app regime is not
stable in the equilibrium sense. It is metastable (LAW-026).

**4.1 Background Mismatch Circulation**

In the vacuum_app regime, background mismatch circulation maintains a
minimum continuation pressure at every locus. The delta operator
(LAW-001) requires ε \> ε_null for continuation candidates to be
generated. The NOT-Axiom guarantees this condition is always met ---
there is always enough mismatch to produce candidate continuation, even
where no persistent structure has yet stabilized.

This means arbitration (LAW-029) never stops operating in the vacuum_app
regime. It merely operates over a nearly flat candidate landscape with
no dominant basin imposing occupancy pressure. The arbitration outcome
is weakly biased, broadly distributed across candidates, and not
self-reinforcing --- so no structure accumulates. But the process runs
continuously.

**4.2 Spontaneous Triadic Closure**

The discrete-continuous transition mechanics (LAW-031) define a
discreteness threshold Θ_D(U): the limit beyond which continuous
gradient variation produces a discrete stabilization event. In the
vacuum_app regime, background mismatch circulation produces continuous
low-amplitude variation in G_A(U) across all loci.

Wherever this variation locally exceeds Θ_D(U) --- through coincident
mismatch accumulation at neighboring loci --- the arbitration operator
selects a candidate that initiates nascent triadic closure. This is not
creation from nothing. It is a threshold crossing by background process
that was never absent.

Once initiated, the nascent triad generates its own interior mismatch
volume. If that volume achieves sufficient reinforcement before
dissipation --- before the propagation front disperses below the
stabilization threshold --- a persistent structure forms. If not, the
event decays back into background circulation without leaving a stable
residue.

> *This provides the framework\'s structural analog for vacuum
> fluctuation: not random quantum events, but threshold crossings in
> continuously running background arbitration. The probability analog is
> the ratio of loci where coincident mismatch accumulation exceeds
> Θ_D(U) to total loci in the vacuum_app regime --- a function of
> background mismatch density, not a primitive probability assignment.*

**4.3 Why Most Events Decay**

A nascent triadic closure requires its interior mismatch volume to
sustain reinforcement long enough for practical closure to occur --- for
circulation to return before propagation dissipates. This is determined
by the finite flux constraint Φ_α \< ∞ (LAW-004) and the transport decay
of NavT (LAW-003).

In the vacuum_app regime, there is no neighboring basin providing
occupancy pressure to bias arbitration toward the nascent structure. The
nascent triad must self-reinforce from scratch against a flat
surrounding landscape. Most threshold crossings fail this test --- the
mismatch accumulation that triggered the event disperses before the
triadic interior stabilizes. Only events where the initial mismatch
surplus is large enough and spatially coherent enough to sustain the
interior through its first recursive cycle produce stable structures.

The vacuum_app regime is therefore punctuated by transient threshold
crossings, the vast majority of which decay without trace. Persistent
structure is the rare outcome --- not the default --- consistent with
the observed sparseness of matter relative to apparent empty space.

**5. Arbitration Topology**

Geometry_app is not fundamentally metric. Observable geometry emerges
from the organization of continuation-selection constraints across
recursive admissibility space --- from arbitration topology.

At each locus α, the arbitration operator Arb_A(Q_α) (LAW-029) selects
continuation candidates from Q_α under recursive bias from local
mismatch asymmetry, basin reinforcement history, admissibility occupancy
pressure, continuation transport decay, and neighboring coherence
structures. This produces a topological organization of admissibility
preference pathways across the orientation array.

Under this interpretation:

**Geodesic_app** corresponds to preferential admissible continuation
pathways --- the routes through the arbitration topology that candidates
consistently favor under occupancy pressure.

**Curvature_app** corresponds to gradients in arbitration bias intensity
--- how rapidly the preference structure changes across neighboring
loci.

**Locality** corresponds to recursive accessibility constraint structure
--- what is reachable from a given locus under finite flux and
admissibility bounds.

Geometry is therefore observable arbitration organization emerging from
ordered recursive continuation --- not a background metric into which
structures are placed.

**6. Admissibility Occupancy Pressure**

Stabilized coherence basins do not consume literal substance. They
generate admissibility occupancy pressure: the narrowing of continuation
freedom caused by recursive reinforcement saturation.

As coherent basins stabilize, reinforcement pathways intensify,
admissibility margins narrow, continuation flexibility decreases, and
arbitration becomes increasingly biased toward basin-aligned
trajectories. Neighboring loci experience reduced admissible traversal
diversity, elevated cost for non-aligned candidates, and increasing
continuation anisotropy.

Occupancy pressure propagates outward via NavT transport (LAW-003)
subject to finite flux constraint Φ_α \< ∞ (LAW-004), attenuating with
orientation-array distance. The gradient of this attenuation --- G_A(U)
varying continuously across the accessibility domain --- is what
arbitration topology translates into curvature_app, which then projects
into gravity_app.

Gravity_app is therefore not produced by attraction. It emerges from
recursively propagated admissibility occupancy pressure shaping the
arbitration topology across continuous continuation space.
