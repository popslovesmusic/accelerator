This is the minimal operational form.

Constraint-map form
Let the constrained reduction operator be \mathcal RUC}_{K_t}. Then
Ωt+1=RUCKt(Ωt,Rt,εt)\boxed{ \Omega_{t+1} = \mathcal RUC_{K_t}(\Omega_t,R_t,\varepsilon_t) }Ωt+1​=RUCKt​​(Ωt​,Rt​,εt​)​

Ωt+1⪯Ωt\boxed{ \Omega_{t+1}\preceq \Omega_t }Ωt+1​⪯Ωt​​
