## 1. Nested Polarity Equivalence
We define the nested equivalence relation:
\[
(\mathcal{E} \neq 0) \iff_R \delta_a(\mathcal{E} > 0) \iff_R [(-1) \iff_R (+1)]
\]
This asserts that:
*   The primary axiom loop (binding potential constraint presence to realized continuation) is recursively isomorphic to the tension between exclusion ($-1$) and accumulation ($+1$) pathways.
*   The polarities are not external numbers; they represent directed process pathways (exclusion filtration vs. crossing layer accumulation) whose mutual tension drives the recursive cycle.

## 2. Non-Separability Acknowledged
The aspects $[-1]$ and $[+1]$ are strictly non-separable from the core axiom:
*   Ablating the exclusion aspect ($-1$) collapses the admissibility filter ($\delta_a$), reducing the system to random noise.
*   Ablating the accumulation aspect ($+1$) prevents invariant stabilization, leading to the $0$-state collapse.
*   They must be evaluated together as a single whole-relation aspect-binding.
