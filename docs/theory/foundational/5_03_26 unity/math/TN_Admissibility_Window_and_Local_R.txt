X. Conclusion

This note resolves the structural ambiguity between admissibility, selection, and reference. The admissibility window is the constraint domain; selection operates within that domain; local reference is induced by selection. This separation is necessary for the framework to remain internally consistent and for the M-Law derivation to remain valid under the core biconditional expression.
