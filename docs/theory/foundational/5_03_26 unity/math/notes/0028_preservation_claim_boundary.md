# Preservation Claim Boundary

## Status

`GOVERNANCE_DEFINITION_CANDIDATE`

- Related obligation: `OBL-D-001D`
- Claim ceiling: `C1_DEFINED_PROVISIONAL`
- Scope: classify claim forms against the bounded evidence boundary.

## Bounded claim admission

A preservation statement may remain at bounded C1 only when it declares:

1. a finite domain/context set;
2. typed inputs and projection route;
3. explicit witness, trace, and history conditions;
4. recoverable fixtures or outputs;
5. observed failure cases and limitations.

The statement must be phrased as support within the tested model scope.

## Stronger-claim blockers

The following claims remain blocked unless their additional obligations are separately discharged:

```text
forall domains: Pi_D preserves representable distinction
Pi_D is injective
Pi_D is reversible
the projected image preserves complete information
the model is physically valid
```

Bounded success does not lift to any of these forms by repetition or internal consistency alone.

## Boundary rule

The current evidence therefore permits `C1_DEFINED_PROVISIONAL` bounded preservation language and blocks universal, injective, reversible, complete-information, and external-physical language.

## Limits

This note defines a claim boundary; it does not discharge OBL-D-001D or OBL-D-001E and does not promote any claim.
