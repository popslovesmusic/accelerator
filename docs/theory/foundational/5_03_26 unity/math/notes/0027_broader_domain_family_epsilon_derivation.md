# Broader Domain-Family `epsilon_a` Derivation

## Status

`ANALYSIS_CANDIDATE`

- Governing obligation: `OBL-D-001E`
- Claim ceiling: `C1_DEFINED_PROVISIONAL`
- Scope: finite explicitly typed domain families and admissibility rules.

## Supported family rules

The bounded derivation recognizes only declared rule/family pairs:

```text
TYPE_AFFECT_EFFECT + positive_nonzero
TYPE_INDEXED_CLOSURE + positive_integer
TYPE_CONTINUATION + positive_resolution
```

For a supported pair, raw distinctions are filtered by the declared rule and `epsilon_{a,C}` is the exact minimum of the resulting nonempty set. Unsupported family/rule pairs are rejected rather than evaluated under a fallback rule.

## Family interpretation

The family labels are typed model descriptors, not claims that physical domains reduce to these categories. The result tests whether the derivation contract can be reused across distinct declared admissibility organizations while preserving explicit rule dependence.

## Limits

The family rules and descriptors are finite supplied inputs. This analysis does not derive a universal family taxonomy, establish physical domain correspondence, or discharge OBL-D-001E.
