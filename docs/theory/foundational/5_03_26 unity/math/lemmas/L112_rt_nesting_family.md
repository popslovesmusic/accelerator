# Lemma L112 — RT Nesting, Condition Primacy, and Family Constitution

## 1. Statement
Within these models:
1. **RT Trace-Admissibility:** $X$ is RT if and only if $X$ can constructively trace its dependency lineage to $\text{RT}_{\text{core}} := [(\mathcal{E}\neq0) \iff_R \delta_a(\mathcal{E}>0)]$ without breaking distinction, admissibility, residue closure, or non-collapse. The trace must be constructively produced, and any claimed trace that collapses under ablation does not satisfy this principle.
2. **Conditions Not States:** Participants in RT distinction expressions are process conditions already carrying residue and admissibility context, not static states. Any addressable state $S$ represents a locally addressable configuration of the ongoing process.
3. **Minimum Nesting Form:** $D(*|*)$ alone is not RT, but represents a constraint condition component. The minimum RT-complete nesting form is:
   $$ [D(*|-*) \langle f \rangle_x D(*|+*)] $$
   where $f$ is a declared member of the $\langle*\rangle_x$ family, homomorphic to $\text{RT}_{\text{core}}$.
4. **Family Name constraint:** The $\langle*\rangle_x$ notation names the family of typed meta-relations. It is not itself an operator and may not be applied directly. Every use of a coupling relation must declare which member is instantiated (e.g., $\otimes$, $\iff_R$, $\iff_m$, $\iff_s$) and what $x$ specifies.
5. **Mutual Constitution:** The $\langle*\rangle_x$ family and its primary distinction conditions are mutually constitutive. The left primary (exclusion, $-$) defines the suppression character, and the right primary (addition, $+$) defines the accumulation character. These directional characters determine the family's admissible membership space, while the family member types and bounds the primaries.

## 3. Proof Sketch
All processes are dynamic conditions rather than static substrates. To establish RT-ness, any expression must constructively trace back to the primary axiom $\text{RT}_{\text{core}}$, ensuring that nesting layers preserve the indivisible unit of analysis. The minimum coupled nesting form maps the left condition to constraint (exclusion) and the right condition to continuation (addition), linked by an instantiated family member $f$ under context $x$. Since $\langle*\rangle_x$ is a schema representing these relations, the primaries constitute the space of possible members, while the instantiated relation types the primary coupling. This reciprocal constraint prevents arbitrary syntactic nesting without trace validity.
