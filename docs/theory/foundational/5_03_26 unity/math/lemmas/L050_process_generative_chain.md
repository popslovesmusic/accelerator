# L050 — The Process Generative Chain

## Statement
The Mono-Process Framework organizes the emergence of multiscale structure into a definitive **Generative Chain**:
[
\text{Continuation} \to \text{Recursive Coupling} \to \text{Distinction} \to \text{Residue} \to \text{Stabilized Structure} \to \text{Topology} \to \text{Geometry}
]
Each stage recursively co-conditions the others. Geometry is the final projected persistence-shape of stabilized, constrained relational organization.

## Dependencies
- Lemma L045 (Topology-Geometry Biconditional)
- Lemma L046 (Recursive Coupling Operator)
- Lemma L047 (Stratified Process Ontology)

## Proof (or Proof sketch)
1. Within this framework, all high-level structures are derived projections of the root process.
2. The generative chain provides the sequence of derivation:
   - Continuation (P) + Residue (R) couple via $\in_x$.
   - This coupling resolves into Distinction ($\varepsilon$).
   - Persistent distinction writes new Residue (R).
   - Accumulated residue forms a Basin (Stabilized Structure).
   - Basin organization yields Topology (Connectivity).
   - Topological accessibility projects as Geometry (Space).
3. This sequence ensures that "Geometry" cannot be treated as a background grid; it is the most derived and most projected layer of the process.
