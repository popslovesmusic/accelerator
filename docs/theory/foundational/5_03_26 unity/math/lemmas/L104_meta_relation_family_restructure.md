# Lemma L104: Meta-Relation Family Restructure

## 1. Statement
The projection and composition operators of the Reciprocal Relation ($RT$) are organized as a unified projection family under the parent operator $\langle*\rangle_x$ (Meta-Relation Family).
1. The dependent operators are defined as specialized projections of $\langle*\rangle_x$:
$$\langle*\rangle_x \to \{\otimes, \Leftrightarrow_s, \Leftrightarrow_m, \Leftrightarrow_R\}$$
where:
- $\otimes$ represents the coupling projection governing compose/composition relations.
- $\Leftrightarrow_s$ represents the statistical projection mapping mismatch intensity to statistical realization.
- $\Leftrightarrow_m$ represents the metric projection mapping metric correspondence.
- $\Leftrightarrow_R$ represents the residue projection mapping residue-conditioned closure.
2. All family members inherit constraints from the parent operator $\langle*\rangle_x$. No family member may be treated as an independent primitive without formal governance promotion.

## 3. Proof Sketch
By `L101`, the Reciprocal Relation $RT := [Affect\|Effect \langle*\rangle_x QM\|GR]$ is recursively inseparable. Projections of this relation onto discrete orientations (quantum-analog spin braids) or continuous scales (gravitational attenuation) are indexed by $\langle*\rangle_x$. 
Since composition, statistical behavior, metric structure, and residue updates are distinct aspect projections of this same singular process, their operators ($\otimes$, $\Leftrightarrow_s$, $\Leftrightarrow_m$, $\Leftrightarrow_R$) must share the common algebraic constraints of the parent meta-relation $\langle*\rangle_x$. Decoupling them into isolated primitives violates process unity and aspect-binding inseparability.
