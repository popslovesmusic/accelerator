# L075 — Interaction Classes as Organizational Thresholds

## Statement
Distinct interaction regimes (analogs) emerge as hierarchical organizational projections of $P_\Delta$ based on recursion depth, saturation, and propagation mode. The regimes are defined by their resolution of $P_\Delta$:

1. **weak_force_app:** Discharge of $P_\Delta$ through state transformation (binary systems).
2. **strong_force_app:** Internalization of $P_\Delta$ through recursive triadic confinement.
3. **electromotive_app:** Directional propagation of $P_\Delta$ via self-reinforcing transport channels.
4. **gravity_app:** Cumulative deformation of the arbitration topology via unbounded recursive volume overlap.

1. $P_\Delta$ is the substrate-level driver (L074).

4. Persistent pathways (depth=1, reinforced) allow directed transfer of $P_\Delta$ (electromotive analog).
5. Large-scale accumulation of volume overlap biases the global selection landscape (gravity analog).
