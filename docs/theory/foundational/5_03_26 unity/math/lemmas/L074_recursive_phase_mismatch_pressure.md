# L074 — Recursive Phase Mismatch Pressure ($P_\Delta$)

## Statement
Non-equivalent recursive mismatch relations generate a primitive continuation tension designated **Recursive Phase Mismatch Pressure** ($P_\Delta$). $P_\Delta$ is the substrate-level generative condition for all interaction-analog regimes. For a relational pair $(S_i, S_j)$, the pressure is induced by the ordered relational asymmetry:

$$\Delta_R = D(S_i \| S_j)_{>0} - D(S_j \| S_i)_{>0} \implies P_\Delta$$

$P_\Delta$ must be resolved through an organizational regime (transition, confinement, transport, or deformation) to maintain process stability.

## Dependencies
- Lemma L073 (Operational Directional Imbalance)
- NOT-Axiom ($\varepsilon \neq 0$)

## Proof Sketch
1. Relational distinguishability is inherently asymmetric (L042).
2. The NOT-Axiom ensures sustained mismatch $\varepsilon > 0$.
3. Ordered relational asymmetry $\Delta_R$ creates a directional preference in the arbitration topology (L073).
4. This preference manifests as a continuation tension ($P_\Delta$) that biases the selection of future states $Q_\alpha$.
5. Stable persistence requires the resolution of $P_\Delta$ into a persistent organizational regime.
