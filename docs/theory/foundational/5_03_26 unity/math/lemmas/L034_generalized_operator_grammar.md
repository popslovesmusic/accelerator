# L034 — Generalized Operator Grammar ($\Leftrightarrow_x$)

## Statement
The core process relation $(\mathcal{E} \neq 0) \Leftrightarrow_x \delta(\mathcal{E} > 0)$ is governed by a family of admissibility-orientation operators where the subscript $x$ specifies the regime-dependent selection rule (e.g., residue-history $R$, phase-coherence $\phi$, or deviation-pressure $\delta$). This operator grammar provides the meta-bridge relating disparate process domains through consistent structural continuation rules.

## Dependencies
- Definitions: `operator_family`, `admissibility_selection_regime`
- Assumptions: Admissibility is context-sensitive and determined by the local orientation frame.
- Prior lemmas: L005 (Residue-Conditioned Closure)

## Proof (or Proof sketch)
1. Let $\mathcal{E}$ be a relational mismatch signal.
2. Let $\delta$ be an additive perturbation generating distinguishability.
3. The operator $\Leftrightarrow_x$ maps the existence of mismatch to the actualization of distinguishable continuation.
4. If $x=R$, the selection depends on the historical residue $R$ (Residue-mediated coupling).
5. If $x=\phi$, the selection depends on relative phase coherence (Phase-mediated relation).
6. The family $\{\Leftrightarrow_x\}$ preserves the monistic process root while allowing domain-specific projections.
