# L073 — Operational Directional Imbalance ($\Delta_R$)

## Statement
Realized mismatch is the operational directional imbalance between recursively coupled states. Formally, for states $S_1$ and $S_2$:

$$\Delta_R = D(S1\|S2)_{>0} - D(S2\|S1)_{>0}$$

$\Delta_R$ is the directional surplus that drives orientation, arbitration bias, and emergent curvature tendency.

## Dependencies
- Lemma L042 (Directional Distinguishability Asymmetry)
- TN-v05 (Gravity Refinement)

## Proof Sketch
1. Relational ordering is fundamentally non-equivalent ($D(S1\|S2) \neq D(S2\|S1)$).
2. The directional surplus $\Delta_R$ produces admissibility anisotropy.
3. This anisotropy is the necessary condition for the emergence of `curvature_app`.
4. When $\Delta_R \to 0$, arbitration landscapes flatten and the system trends toward reversible symmetry.
