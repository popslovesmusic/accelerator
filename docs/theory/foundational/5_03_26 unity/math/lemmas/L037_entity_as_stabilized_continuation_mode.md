# L037 — Entity as Stabilized Continuation Mode

## Statement
A persistent "entity" or "structure" is not an independent substance inserted into space, but a localized region of the recursive process that has achieved stable orientational locking. An entity is "reality organized entity-wise" through maintained orientational relations ($ -(i) $) under continuous admissibility constraints.

## Dependencies
- Definitions: `knot_stabilization`, `continuation_pathway`, `entity`
- Assumptions: Non-separability of structure from process; reification of entities as independent primitives is forbidden.
- Prior lemmas: L036 (Ratchet Deformation / Knot Insight)

## Proof (or Proof sketch)
1. Let $\mathcal{P}$ be the root recursive process.
2. A localized region $S \subset \mathcal{P}$ achieves stabilization when its internal orientational relations $-(i)_S$ become recursively self-reinforcing.
3. This stabilization (knotting) leaves a persistent residue $R_S$ that deforms the local admissibility manifold.
4. The "persistence" of $S$ is the ongoing successful evaluation of the update rule $\Delta \in A(R_S)$ over multiple cycles.
5. If the orientational lock $-(i)_S$ is broken or the residue $R_S$ decays, the "entity" dissolves back into the unresolved process.
6. Therefore, the entity is a *mode of continuation* of the whole process, not a separate part.
