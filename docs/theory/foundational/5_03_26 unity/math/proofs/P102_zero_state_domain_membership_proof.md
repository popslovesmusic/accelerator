# Proof P102: Zero-State Domain Membership Proof

## 1. Goal
Provide a structural justification for the inadmissibility of the zero-state within the distinction domain and the asymmetric recoupling requirement.

## 2. Uses
- `L102`: Zero-State Domain Membership
- `T001`: The 3-Peak Rule

## 3. Proof
1. By the 3-Peak Rule (`T001`), structural stability and distinction require $N \ge 3$ crossings. 
2. In the zero-state, by definition, $D = 0$, implying $N = 0$. Since $0 < 3$, the zero-state has no stable crossings, hence it lacks distinction and degrees of freedom.
3. Therefore, the zero-state is not a member of $D_{\text{domain}}$.
4. Breaking this symmetry requires generating $N \ge 3$ crossings (asymmetry), which is equivalent to the first asymmetric recoupling ($0\_DOF \to D > 0$), establishing the first distinction.
