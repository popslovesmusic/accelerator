# Proof P104: Meta-Relation Family Restructure Proof

## 1. Goal
Provide a structural justification for the dependency mapping of the projection operators onto the parent family $\langle*\rangle_x$.

## 2. Uses
- `L104`: Meta-Relation Family Restructure
- `L101`: Universal Meta-Relation inside RT

## 3. Proof
1. By `L101`, all domain-scale-orientation updates are mediated by the coupling operator $\langle*\rangle_x$ inside the inseparable expression $RT$.
2. Any specialized relation (such as coupling composition $\otimes$, statistical correspondence $\Leftrightarrow_s$, metric mapping $\Leftrightarrow_m$, or residue closure $\Leftrightarrow_R$) is defined strictly by restricting the scale, domain, or orientation parameters of the universal projection $\Pi$ of $RT$.
3. Because the domain of these projections is a subset of the domain of $RT$, any algebraic constraint or limit on $\langle*\rangle_x$ (such as non-invertibility `L093` or the tau criterion `L094`) must propagate to $\otimes$, $\Leftrightarrow_s$, $\Leftrightarrow_m$, and $\Leftrightarrow_R$.
4. Thus, the dependent operators cannot be treated as isolated primitives.
