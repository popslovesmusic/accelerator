# Primitive Relational Condition, Admissible Distinction, and Symmetric Biconditional

This document records the formal refinement of the foundational ontology of the Mono-Process Framework. It shifts the ontological primitive from distinction itself to the relational condition permitting distinction.

---

## 1. The Foundational Chain
Ontological emergence proceeds along the following relational path:
\[
(*|*) \Leftrightarrow_S \delta_a(\mathcal{E} \neq 0) \to (A \mid E)
\]

### 1.1 Reading
1.  The primitive relational condition is symmetrically coupled with admissible distinction.
2.  Once admissible distinction exists, the Affect-Effect gradient becomes operational.

---

## 2. Term and Symbol Definitions

### 2.1 Primitive Relational Condition: `(*|*)`
The minimal relational condition permitting the possibility of operational distinction. It is not itself a distinction, observation, or identity. It is prior to distinction.

### 2.2 Existence of Distinction: `E≠0`
An existential statement asserting that at least one operational distinction exists within the admissible relational domain.

### 2.3 Admissibility Operator: `δa`
Evaluates whether relational conditions satisfy the requirements for operational distinction.

### 2.4 Symmetric Relational Biconditional: `<-S->` (written as $\Leftrightarrow_S$)
A domain-specific biconditional expressing mutual relational necessity rather than classical logical equivalence. It is characterized by:
-   **Symmetry:** Mutual necessity between the primitive condition and the admissibility of distinction.
-   **Non-causality:** Neither term causes the other; they are co-present.
-   **Domain Relativity:** Scoped within the active relational regime.

### 2.5 Affect-Effect Gradient: `(A|E)`
The primary emergent gradient resulting from admissible distinction:
-   **Affect ($A$):** Relational influence, tendency, or pressure.
-   **Effect ($E$):** Realized action or consequence resulting from admissible affect.

---

## 3. Operator Philosophy
Operators are relational conditions. They primarily function through **exclusion** rather than construction. Operational structure emerges by excluding incompatible relational configurations until admissible distinction remains.

---

## 4. Symmetry and Observation
1.  **Bounded Symmetry (`<S>`):** Reference-bounded symmetry.
2.  **Unbounded Symmetry (`>S<`):** Operationally unrestricted symmetry.
3.  **Observation Principle:** Observation resides in the relational operator rather than either symmetry state.

---

## 5. Logical Progression
1.  `(*|*)` — Primitive relational condition.
2.  `(*|*) <-S-> δa(E≠0)` — Primitive condition and admissible distinction are mutually necessary.
3.  `(A|E)` — Formation of the Affect-Effect relational gradient.
