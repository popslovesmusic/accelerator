**1. Scope of Extension**

This technical note extends the procedural gravity ontology developed in
TN_Gravity_Refinement_v05 into a broader analog investigation concerning
interaction classes. The goal is not force unification in the physical
sense. The goal is to investigate whether apparently distinct
interaction regimes may emerge as hierarchical organizational
projections of a single deeper procedural substrate.

The proposed substrate-level primitive is admissible detectable mismatch
D(S_i \| S_j) \> 0, with ordered relational asymmetry treated as
foundational:

D(S1\|S2) \> 0 ≠ D(S2\|S1) \> 0

Δ_R = D(S1\|S2)\>0 − D(S2\|S1)\>0

This note proposes that four interaction-like regimes ---
weak_force_app, strong_force_app, electromotive_app, and gravity_app ---
emerge as distinct recursive organizations of phase mismatch pressure
P_Δ operating under different continuation structures, recursion scales,
and admissibility organizations.

> *All interaction analogs discussed here are procedural projections
> developed for structural comparison and ontological investigation.
> None is claimed equivalent to Standard Model interactions, quantum
> chromodynamics, electroweak theory, or experimentally validated
> gravitation.*

**2. Recursive Phase Mismatch Pressure**

Non-equivalent recursive mismatch relations generate a primitive
continuation tension designated recursive phase mismatch pressure P_Δ.
This is not classical force, energetic substance, or metric curvature.
It is the continuation tension generated by ordered admissibility
asymmetry --- the pressure that must be resolved by some organizational
regime or else drive the system toward transition, decay, or
reconfiguration.

D(S_i \| S_j) \> 0 → Δ_R → P_Δ

P_Δ is the substrate-level generative condition from which all four
interaction analogs emerge. The specific organizational regime through
which P_Δ is resolved determines which interaction class results.
Different resolutions produce different continuation behaviors ---
confinement, transport, decay, or distributed deformation --- at
different recursion scales and overlap densities.

**3. Organizational Thresholds: Binary and Triadic Regimes**

**3.1 Binary Regime --- weak_force_app**

Binary mismatch systems possess distinguishability, directional
relation, and admissibility asymmetry, but lack sufficient recursive
closure depth for stable persistence locking. The two-node system cannot
generate the recursive return and reinforcement circulation required for
triadic coherence. P_Δ therefore cannot be resolved into stable
confinement --- it must be discharged through transformation.

Binary systems consequently tend toward transition, oscillation, decay,
instability mediation, and reconfiguration. The system resolves its
mismatch pressure by changing state rather than holding it.

weak_force_app = transition mediation within continuation systems below
triadic closure threshold

The weak_force analog is therefore continuation transformation
organization --- the procedural regime governing how P_Δ is discharged
when recursive depth is insufficient for locking. It is not confinement.
It is the organized release of unresolvable mismatch tension.

The instability of binary regimes is not a failure mode --- it is
structurally expected and structurally productive. Decay events in this
regime are admissibility-threshold crossings (LAW-031) that redirect
mismatch pressure into new continuation organizations rather than
absorbing it.

**3.2 Triadic Regime --- strong_force_app**

Triadic systems introduce recursive return, reinforcement circulation,
practical closure, and persistence stabilization. The triad is the
minimum recursive coherence lock: the smallest continuation organization
capable of sustaining P_Δ internally rather than discharging it through
transformation.

The triadic interior --- a recursive mismatch volume (Section 4) ---
operates at maximal admissibility occupancy pressure. The three
reinforcement nodes are coupled such that separating any pair requires
work against the full interior circulation. Dispersal of one node
increases P_Δ across the remaining pair, driving a restoring
continuation pressure that resists separation.

strong_force_app = recursive phase coupling maintaining triadic mismatch
coherence against dispersal

The gluon analog is not interpreted as a literal exchanged particle. It
is the active recursive coupling process --- the continuation transport
maintaining interior coherence between mismatch nodes. Under this
interpretation the coupling does not travel between nodes; it is the
ongoing arbitration process sustaining the triadic interior.

Confinement emerges because separation requires crossing an
admissibility boundary (LAW-005) beyond which the interior mismatch
volume cannot sustain its circulation regime. The system undergoes basin
collapse (LAW-024) rather than permit indefinite separation --- the
coherence cavity fails and the released P_Δ immediately reorganizes into
new triadic closures.

**4. Recursive Mismatch Volumes**

The triad is not an edge-defined enclosure. Because the substrate is
continuous and mismatch-mediated, the interior of every triadic closure
regime participates in recursive continuation dynamics. The triad is a
recursive mismatch volume whose interior contains recursive
admissibility structure, continuation arbitration, orientation
gradients, propagation redistribution, and active mismatch circulation.

The triadic peaks function as recursive reinforcement maxima, coherence
anchors, and orientation intensification regions. The interior is not
passive --- it is the active regime where continuation candidates are
generated, arbitrated, and selectively reinforced against dispersal.

When recursive mismatch volumes overlap, coherence cavities emerge,
admissibility gradients redistribute, arbitration topologies reorganize,
and larger persistence basins stabilize (LAW-011, LAW-024). The
interaction analogs operate at different overlap scales:
strong_force_app at the single-volume interior, electromotive_app across
transport corridors, gravity_app across distributed multi-volume
accumulation.

**5. Electromotive_app --- Directional Propagation Organization**

The electromotive_app regime occupies a structural position that prior
versions described but did not mechanize. The central question is: what
continuation organization produces directional propagation and
oscillatory transport rather than confinement locking or transformation
discharge?

**5.1 The Transport Channel Regime**

The answer is a persistent transport channel C_P (LAW-012) that achieves
neither triadic confinement nor binary decay. This is a distinct
organizational threshold between the two.

In the triadic regime, P_Δ is resolved by recursive closure --- mismatch
pressure is held internally through reinforcement circulation. In the
binary regime, P_Δ is resolved by transformation --- mismatch pressure
is discharged through state change. In the transport channel regime, P_Δ
is resolved by propagation --- mismatch pressure is transferred
directionally along a reinforced pathway without closing into a basin
and without decaying into transformation.

The transport channel C_P sustains phase coherence across
orientation-array distance. It does not trap its mismatch --- it moves
it. The channel is reinforced by the passage of mismatch itself: each
propagation event strengthens the pathway for the next (LAW-015),
creating a self-sustaining directional corridor.

electromotive_app = directional mismatch propagation sustained by
self-reinforcing transport channels

**5.2 Oscillatory Character**

The oscillatory nature of electromotive_app emerges from the channel\'s
boundary conditions. A transport channel operating between two
mismatch-asymmetric regions propagates P_Δ from the higher-Δ_R region
toward the lower --- the directional asymmetry of the channel itself is
governed by the ordered relational asymmetry that generated P_Δ in the
first place.

When the channel reaches a region of comparable mismatch density,
propagation does not simply stop. The transported P_Δ generates a local
continuation pressure that reflects back along the channel --- the
channel becomes a resonant corridor, sustaining oscillatory mismatch
transfer between its anchor regions. This is the framework\'s account of
the oscillatory character of electromagnetic-analog propagation: not a
wave in a medium, but resonant continuation transport in a
self-reinforcing pathway.

**5.3 Relation to Arbitration Topology**

Electromotive_app interacts with the arbitration topology differently
than gravity_app. Gravity_app deforms the arbitration landscape globally
--- it biases the candidate set at every locus within its accessibility
domain by accumulating occupancy pressure through volume overlap.
Electromotive_app does not deform the landscape globally. It establishes
a local directional preference structure along the channel pathway,
leaving the surrounding arbitration topology relatively unaffected.

This distinction explains the structural difference in range and
coupling character: gravity_app accumulates with every additional volume
overlap and is never screened; electromotive_app is pathway-specific and
subject to channel competition (LAW-014) --- a stronger nearby channel
can redirect transport, producing the analog of screening.

**6. Gravity_app --- Large-Scale Arbitration Deformation**

The gravity_app regime is developed in full in
TN_Gravity_Refinement_v05. The essential character within the
interaction hierarchy is this: gravity_app is the only regime that
accumulates without saturation.

Strong_force_app saturates at the triadic interior --- maximal occupancy
pressure is reached within the coherence volume. Electromotive_app is
channeled --- transport is pathway-specific and subject to screening.
Weak_force_app is local --- binary decay events are self-terminating.
Gravity_app has no saturation mechanism because it operates through
volume overlap accumulation rather than interior pressure. Each
additional recursive mismatch volume that overlaps with a region adds
incrementally to the admissibility gradient G_A(U) across that region\'s
accessibility domain. The accumulation is unbounded in principle.

gravity_app = large-scale arbitration deformation from unbounded
recursive volume overlap accumulation

Gravitational weakness is therefore the structural consequence of
operating at the largest recursion scale with the lowest per-volume
contribution. Each volume\'s contribution to G_A(U) at a distant locus
is heavily attenuated through transport decay, occupancy dilution,
reconstruction compression, and geometry projection. But the
contributions sum --- and because there is no screening mechanism, they
sum across all volumes within the accessibility domain.

**8. Strong_force_app ⇔\_x Gravity_app: Reciprocal Scale Coupling**

The relationship between strong_force_app and gravity_app is not one-way
derivation. It is a recursive structural conditioning relation:

strong_force_app ⇔\_x gravity_app

This requires unpacking in both directions.

**8.1 Local to Global: How Confinement Contributes to Arbitration
Structure**

Every stabilized triadic closure contributes a recursive mismatch volume
to the substrate. That volume generates admissibility occupancy pressure
that propagates outward through NavT transport (LAW-003), attenuating
with distance but never vanishing within finite accessibility range. It
contributes incrementally to the admissibility gradient G_A(U) across
its surrounding region.

The global arbitration topology --- the organized preference structure
that constitutes geometry_app and projects into gravity_app --- is
therefore the accumulated superposition of contributions from every
stable triadic closure in the accessibility domain. Strong_force_app, by
maintaining stable triadic coherence, supplies the local recursive
mismatch volumes from which global arbitration structure is built.
Without confinement, there are no stable volumes. Without stable
volumes, there is no accumulating gradient. Without accumulating
gradient, gravity_app vanishes.

This is the upward direction of the coupling: local confinement
coherence is the substrate from which global admissibility organization
emerges.

**8.2 Global to Local: How Arbitration Structure Constrains
Confinement**

The global admissibility gradient G_A(U) is not a passive background. It
constitutes the arbitration topology within which new triadic closures
must stabilize. The downward constraint propagation Constrain(S_j → S_i)
(LAW-030) means that the global coherence structure --- the accumulated
gravity_app regime --- narrows the admissibility options available at
local loci.

Practically: a region of high admissibility occupancy pressure ---
strong gravity_app curvature --- is a region where the arbitration
candidate set for new triadic closures is already significantly narrowed
by existing basin contributions. New confinement structures that align
with the prevailing arbitration topology are preferentially stabilized;
those that oppose it face elevated continuation cost and reduced
persistence support.

The global admissibility structure therefore constrains which local
confinement configurations can stabilize and persist. High-curvature
gravity_app regions are regions of constrained triadic closure --- the
confinement landscape is shaped by the accumulated history of prior
confinement.

**8.3 The Reciprocal Loop**

The full coupling is therefore:

Stable triadic closures (strong_force_app) generate mismatch volumes
whose overlap accumulates into the admissibility gradient (gravity_app).
The admissibility gradient constrains which triadic closures can
stabilize in each region. The distribution of stable triadic closures
determines the next state of the admissibility gradient. The loop is
continuous, scale-coupled, and mutually conditioning.

> *Strong_force_app supplies local recursive coherence pressure.
> Gravity_app supplies global admissibility organization. Neither is
> derivable from the other alone --- they are co-emergent projections of
> P_Δ at different recursion scales, reciprocally coupled through the
> orientation array.*

This is not a claim that gravity is the strong force. It is the proposal
that both emerge from the same mismatch-pressure substrate and are
coupled through the shared orientation array that both organize.

**9. Compliance and Classification**

**Exploratory status:** This note is an analog investigation, not a
physical theory. All interaction classes are procedural projections for
structural comparison and ontological refinement.

**Non-equivalence:** No claims of equivalence with Standard Model
interactions, quantum chromodynamics, electroweak theory, or
experimentally validated gravitation.

**Provisional:** The electromotive_app mechanism and the
strong_force_app ⇔\_x gravity_app coupling are proposed structural
accounts pending formal derivation from Codex operators.

**Falsifiable within the framework:** A transport channel regime that
cannot be distinguished from triadic confinement or binary decay would
falsify the three-threshold organizational structure. A gravity_app
regime that does not accumulate from triadic volume overlap would
falsify the upward direction of the reciprocal coupling.
