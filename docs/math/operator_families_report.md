# Technical Report: Operator Families and Subscripts in the Mono-Process Framework

## 1. Executive Summary
This report formalizes the classification and usage of **Operator Families** and **Subscript Grammar** within the Mono-Process Framework (MPF). It establishes that all operators are derived from the foundational recursive inseparability relation **(ℰ≠0) ⇔_R δ(ℰ>0)** and are governed as restricted-domain projections of the single process.

## 2. The Core Connective Family (⇔_x)
The connective **⇔** represents the primary recursive aspect-binding between distinction (ℰ) and continuation (δ). It is not an operator in the conventional sense but a relational grammar.

### 2.1 Foundational Subscripts
- **⇔_R (Residue-Conditioned):** The canonical form. Denotes that the relationship carries its own history (residue) as an active deformation of future admissibility.
- **⇔_x (General Admissibility):** A family of co-conditioning relations where **x** denotes a specific deformation-regime or local grammar set.
- **⇔_xa (Asymmetric Relational Exchange):** A subset of the **x** family that specifically governs non-reciprocal pressure distributions between triad-node neighbors.

## 3. The Projection Operator Family (Π_x)
Projection operators transform the simultaneous whole-relation of the process into restricted symbolic domains (classical math/logic).

## 4. The Functional Operator Set
Derived from specific aspects of the core relation, these operators execute localized process transformations.

### 4.1 Transition and Transport
- **δ (Continuation):** Formalizes the selection of an admissible next state.
- **NavT (Transport):** Models the propagation of continuation consequences across the interaction domain (CSI).
- **Π_A (Admissibility):** A projection of residue-binding that filters for allowed process increments.

### 4.2 Reconstruction and Arbitration
- **Ξ (Reconstruction):** Attempts to invert process history by reading the residue field.
- **Arb_A (Arbitration):** Resolves multi-valued selection conflicts under budget constraints.
- **T_E (Topology Evolution):** Maps stabilized topological structures between process states.

## 5. Subscript Grammar & Semantic Roles
Subscripts in MPF function as **descriptors of constraint** and **domain identifiers**.

### 5.1 DESCRIPTORS (Conditioning)
- **_R**: Residue-conditioned / History-bound.
- **_Dom**: Dominant / Symmetry-broken reference (e.g., -(i)_Dom).
- **_align**: Alignment-dependent.
- **_T**: Transition-dependent.

### 5.2 DOMAINS (Targeting)
- **_app**: Apparent / Projected appearance (e.g., space_app, matter_app, energy_app).
- **_proc**: Internal process-logic reference.
- **_proj**: Restricted-depth projection reference.
- **_analog**: Explicit analog/metaphorical label.

## 6. The Imaginary Operator Family (L064)
A specialized set of regulators representing phase-state regulators for stable relational negotiation.
- **Quadrants:** {--, ++, -+, +-}
- **Role:** Preventing total synchronization collapse into degenerate symmetry.

## 7. Governance and Usage Rules
1. **No Independent Existence:** Operators and their outputs MUST NOT be treated as independent primitives.
2. **Mandatory Loss Accounting:** Usage of Π_x requires explicit declaration of features lost during projection.
3. **Subscript Strictness:** Subscripts are NOT optional decorators; they define the admissibility regime of the term.
4. **Subordination:** All projected forms (=, →, ↔) remain subordinate to the ⇔_R source relation.
