## 3. Law Statements
- **No primitive geometry:** geometry is not treated as an underlying container, metric substrate, or absolute coordinate frame.
- **Metric-like structure:** when present, is derived from persistent accessibility/reconciliation relations and is not assumed beforehand.
- **Topology Precedence:** Accessibility relations and admissibility constraints determine the geometric appearance, not the other way around.

## 4. Governance Constraints
- **No Physics Claim:** This law defines mathematical apparent-geometry projection and does not claim to describe physical spacetime geometry.
- **No Spacetime Metric:** This law does not claim physical metric spacetime or gravitational equivalence.
- **No Absolute Coordinate Frame:** Geometry is relational and local to the cluster `G_U`.
