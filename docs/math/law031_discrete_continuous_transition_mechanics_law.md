# Law-031: Discrete-Continuous Transition Mechanics Law

## 1. Definition
The **Discrete-Continuous Transition Mechanics Law** formalizes the relationship between continuous process gradients (admissibility, reinforcement, perturbation, budget) and discrete continuation events. It defines how threshold-crossing events produce discrete stabilization, pruning, or regime shifts without assuming physical quantization or quantum theory.

## 2. Formal Statement
Within the recursive continuation framework:

- **Orientation Array**: {-(i)_α}
- **Continuous State Candidate ($G_A(U)$)**: The continuous gradient of admissibility margin, reinforcement support, or budget pressure across a region $U$.
- **Threshold Candidate ($\Theta_D(U)$)**: The operational limit or boundary condition beyond which the current continuation structure must reorganize.
- **Discrete Event Candidate ($E_D$)**: A member of the set $\{stabilize, prune, bifurcate, collapse, merge, redirect, regime\_shift\}$.
- **Threshold Crossing Condition**: $E_D$ occurs when $G_A(U) \ge \Theta_D(U)$ (or $\le$, depending on the specific threshold type) under declared admissibility, reinforcement, or budget tolerance.
- **Stabilization Quantization ($Q_{stab}(U)$)**: The partitioning of continuation outcomes into distinct discrete states induced by threshold-crossing events.
- **Continuity Preservation Clause**: Underlying continuous variation ($G_A$) is preserved as gradient structure even when the resulting operational outcomes ($E_D$) appear discrete.
- **Non-Physical Discreteness Clause**: Discrete events are treated as emergent operational stabilization outcomes. This law does not claim to describe physical quantization, quantum discreteness, or a universal discrete ontology.

## 3. Core Principles
- **Emergent Discreteness**: Discreteness arises from the interaction of continuous gradients with sharp admissibility thresholds.
- **Threshold Mediated Selection**: Selection (LAW-001) is the primary mechanism through which continuous possibilities are compressed into discrete outcomes.
- **Gradient Persistence**: The framework maintains the underlying continuous gradients as the "engine" driving discrete reorganization events.
- **No Primitive Discretization**: Discreteness is not a background property of the orientation array but a consequence of recursive stabilization.

## 4. Governance & Limits
- **No Physics Claim**: This law defines transition mechanics within the Mono-Process Framework and does not claim to describe physical quantization (e.g., Planck scale) or quantum mechanics.
- **No Quantum Mechanics Recovery**: MPF does not assume or derive quantum theory through this law.
- **No Physical Quantization**: Mapping discrete events to physical particles, charges, or energy levels is explicitly blocked.
- **No Universal Discrete Ontology**: The framework remains procedurally monistic and does not assume a background discrete grid or atomistic substance.
- **No Erasure of Continuity**: Discreteness must be shown to emerge from underlying continuity; continuous gradients must not be suppressed.
- **Threshold Dependency**: All discrete events must be traceable to specific, measurable threshold-crossing conditions.

## 5. Failure Modes
- **Physical Quantization Overclaim**: Mapping threshold-driven stabilization directly to the quantization of physical fields.
- **Quantum Mechanics Leakage**: Using quantum concepts (e.g., superposition, wave-function collapse) to explain transition mechanics.
- **Universal Discretization Overclaim**: Asserting that all process structures are fundamentally discrete.
- **Continuity Erasure**: Modeling discrete events in a way that ignores or destroys the underlying continuous gradients.
- **Threshold Without Tolerance**: Modeling thresholds as absolute points without accounting for the required admissibility or reinforcement tolerances.
- **Discrete Event Without Admissibility**: Describing stabilization events that bypass the core admissibility window constraints.
- **Primitive Discrete Substance Reintroduction**: Treating discrete "objects" or "states" as primitives rather than emergent organizations.
- **Physics Claim Leakage**: Citing transition mechanics as a validation of physical discretization theory.
