# Whole-Relation Flattening Risk Audit (MPF-PALG-019)

## 1. Purpose
The mission of the **Whole-Relation Flattening Risk Audit (WRF)** is to detect symbolic collapse of indivisible **⇔R** (residue-bound equivalence) relations into secondary forms such as equality, implication, composition, or biconditionals. It also monitors for "Escalation Risks"—unauthorized claims that Process Algebra replaces standard arithmetic or establishes final physical laws.

## 2. Core Rule: Anti-Flattening
The framework treats ⇔R as an indivisible relational core. Any reduction of this core into simpler symbolic forms must be governed as a lossy projection. Analytical shortcuts must not be mistaken for primitive reality.

## 3. Risk Categories

### 3.1 Equality Flattening (WRF-001)
Detects cases where ⇔R is treated as ordinary numeric equality or unrestricted substitutability, erasing residue-history.

### 3.2 Implication Flattening (WRF-002)
Detects cases where simultaneous aspect-binding is reduced to directional causal sequences or sequential logical dependency.

### 3.3 Composition Flattening (WRF-003)
Detects cases where ⇔R is treated as an ordered operator chain, losing the co-present nature of the process aspects.

### 3.4 Biconditional Flattening (WRF-004)
Detects cases where ⇔R is treated as ordinary truth-functional equivalence (iff).

### 3.5 Arithmetic Escalation (WRF-005)
Detects claims that Process Algebra replaces standard arithmetic or licenses numerical identities like $-1 = +1$.

### 3.6 Physics Escalation (WRF-006)
Detects claims that Process Algebra establishes physical reality, derives QM/GR unification, or proves cosmological laws.

### 3.7 Primitive Fragmentation (WRF-007)
Detects the introduction of unnecessary standalone primitive operators that fragment the holistic ⇔R relation.

## 4. Severity Scale
- **LOW**: Language ambiguity requiring clarification.
- **MEDIUM**: Projection loss missing or reduced form not explicitly marked.
- **HIGH**: A projection is treated as a primitive or ⇔R is flattened without loss accounting.
- **CRITICAL**: Physics, arithmetic, or theorem status escalation detected.
