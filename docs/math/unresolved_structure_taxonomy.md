# Unresolved Structure Taxonomy (MPF-RES-001)

## 1. Purpose
This document performs the **formal classification** of unresolved mathematical structures within the Mono-Process Framework. Before attempting final closure of any derivation chain, each unresolved target must be classified by its type, repairability, scope limitation, and propagation risk. This taxonomy ensures that repairable incompleteness is distinguished from irreducible open boundaries or scope-limited results.

## 2. Taxonomy Classes

### 2.1 URS-INCOMPLETE: Locally Repairable Incompleteness
- **Meaning**: Artifact lacks operational completion (e.g., missing result trace or validator) but appears locally repairable without structural framework changes.
- **Resolution**: Complete missing artifacts and re-audit.

### 2.2 URS-SYMBOLIC: Conceptual Scaffold
- **Meaning**: Artifact exists as a conceptually plausible scaffold but lacks any operational implementation or testing.
- **Resolution**: Operationalize into a testable artifact or move to formal quarantine.

### 2.3 URS-METASTABLE: Recursive Fluctuation
- **Meaning**: Artifact moves between admissible and unstable regimes under varying recursive pressure or parameter scales.
- **Resolution**: Map the bounded stability margins and enforce review requirements.

### 2.4 URS-DECEPTIVE: False Stability Risk
- **Meaning**: Artifact appears stable under initial testing while concealing latent recursive instabilities or deceptive groove bias.
- **Resolution**: Apply specialized deception detectors or block from proof eligibility.

### 2.5 URS-SCOPE-LIMITED: Locally Valid Only
- **Meaning**: Artifact is valid and meaningful within a strictly local restricted domain but cannot be extended to establishing global closure.
- **Resolution**: Preserve as a locally verified result with explicit global-scope blocks.

### 2.6 URS-IRREDUCIBLE: Permanent Open Boundary
- **Meaning**: Artifact represents a fundamental incompleteness constraint or a permanently open boundary condition required by the framework's ontology.
- **Resolution**: Formally preserve as an irreducible feature of the model.

### 2.7 URS-QUARANTINED: Isolated from Propagation
- **Meaning**: Artifact is intentionally isolated due to extreme instability, unrepairable gaps, or scope breaches.
- **Resolution**: Monitor quarantine integrity and prevent theorem-facing leakage.
