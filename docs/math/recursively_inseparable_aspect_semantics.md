# Recursively Inseparable Aspect Semantics (MPF-RSEM-011)

## 1. Conceptual Breakthrough: From Logic to Process
The core semantic shift in the Mono-Process Framework is the transition from reading the relation $\iff_R$ as symbolic logic to reading it as an indivisible process relation.

Instead of interpreting:
$$\boxed{ A \iff_R B }$$
as the logical statement:
> "A if and only if B,"

the intended process reading is:
> **"A and B are recursively inseparable aspects of one admissible continuation structure."**

## 2. Location of Meaning
In this semantic category, meaning is not located in:
- The left aspect alone ($A$),
- The right aspect alone ($B$),
- Nor even in their evaluated truth values.

The meaning is located strictly within the **inseparable recursive relation itself**.

## 3. Structural Mandates
- **Recursively Inseparable**: $A$ and $B$ cannot exist or be resolved independently within the framework's core algebra.
- **One Admissible Continuation Structure**: The relation defines the boundary of what can "happen next" (continuation) within a constrained system.
- **Aspect Roles**: $A$ and $B$ are not "objects" but "roles" or "views" of the same underlying structural necessity.

## 4. Governance Status
- **Theorem Status**: NOT_PROVEN

## 5. Forbidden Interpretations
- Reading $\iff_R$ as a mapping between two independent pre-existing entities.
- Treating the relation as a "glue" added to autonomous variables.
- Using truth-value equivalence to claim ontological identity.
