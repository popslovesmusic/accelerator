# Adaptive Incompleteness Stabilization Layer (MPF-RES-007)

## 1. Purpose
This document establishes the **Adaptive Incompleteness Stabilization Layer**. Its primary mission is to stabilize permanently open and irreducible mathematical structures without inducing closure pressure escalation. It treats irreducible openness as a sustainable governed condition rather than a temporary defect, preventing recursive repair obsession and proof absolutism.

## 2. Core Concepts

### 2.1 Bounded Admissibility Persistence
A structure may remain operationally admissible within bounded local conditions indefinitely without progressing toward proof completion. This is a first-class governance principle.

### 2.2 Epistemic Saturation
A state where additional repair attempts or interventions no longer improve admissibility stability and instead increase governance instability risk.

### 2.3 Non-Closure Operationality
Mathematical structures may remain operationally useful for bounded review and simulation purposes without requiring theorem closure or global resolution.

### 2.4 Adaptive Irreducibility
Irreducible structures may evolve in their governance classification (e.g., from `under_stress_test` to `irreducible_stable`) while remaining permanently unresolved in the traditional sense.

## 3. Stabilization Classes
- **bounded_open_stable**: Locally verified structure with acknowledged open boundaries.
- **irreducible_stable**: Formally classified as a fundamental incompleteness feature.
- **persistent_local_admissible**: Stable under recursive pressure but not generalizing.
- **quarantine_stabilized**: Isolated structure that has achieved internal stability within its quarantine.
- **non_closure_operational**: Implementation is robust enough for use despite missing proof.
- **repair_exhausted_preserved**: Further repair attempts blocked due to epistemic saturation.
- **epistemically_saturated**: Systemic state where certainty accumulation is halted.

## 4. Preservation Modes
- **passive_preservation**: Monitoring without active intervention.
- **active_boundary_monitoring**: Continuous verification of local domain adherence.
- **recursive_stability_preservation**: Probing for long-term stability without forcing convergence.
- **non_escalatory_observation**: Allowing monitoring without triggering theorem-authority inheritance.
- **containment_only**: Strict isolation from all propagation.

## 5. Governance Invariants
- **NOT_PROVEN** status must be maintained.
- **STRICTLY_LOCAL_RESTRICTED_DOMAIN** scope is mandatory.
- **NON_PHYSICAL_ANALOG_MODEL** for all simulations.
- **Simulation evidence supports review only**, not proof.
