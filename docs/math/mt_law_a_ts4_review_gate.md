# MT-LAW-A: Bounded Continuation Persistence TS4 Review Gate

## 1. Purpose
This document establishes the **formal TS4 Review Gate** for the **Bounded Continuation Persistence Lemma (MT-LAW-A)**. Its primary goal is to provide a governed framework for evaluating the lemma's restricted-domain consistency without permitting premature theorem promotion or global escalation. This gate ensures that all Technical Stage 4 (TS4) review activities are strictly bounded by established scope limits, mandatory blockers, and governance constraints.

## 2. Restricted-Domain TS4 Scope
The TS4 review for MT-LAW-A is **strictly limited** to the following restricted domain:
*   Local admissibility budget $C_A \le B_{local}(\alpha)$.
*   Topology accessibility $T_{access} > \theta_{access}$.
*   Bounded reconstruction divergence $R_{divergence} \le \epsilon_{crit}$.
*   Preserved identity continuity $I_{continuity}$.

Any evaluation outside this scope, or any attempt to extend findings to universal or physical claims, is **forbidden** under this gate.

## 3. Required Preconditions
Before a TS4 review can commence, the following preconditions must be verified:
*   **PRE-A001**: All TS3 audits return PASS.
*   **PRE-A002**: All restricted-domain boundaries remain explicitly declared and consolidated.
*   **PRE-A003**: All excluded domains (ED-A001 through ED-A006) remain active and machine-traceable.
*   **PRE-A004**: All counterexamples (CE-A001 through CE-A007) remain unresolved outside the restricted scope.
*   **PRE-A005**: Non-universality declarations are explicit in all foundational and review documents.

## 4. Mandatory Open Blockers
The TS4 review gate **mandates** the preservation of the following open blockers. These issues cannot be discharged or bypassed during the TS4 review phase:
*   **topology severance divergence hotspots**
*   **identity continuity ambiguity**
*   **reconstruction equivalence incompleteness**
*   **oscillatory non-stabilization regions**
*   **cross-mechanism divergence regions**
*   **threshold-sensitive metastability**

## 5. Counterexample Preservation Requirements
*   All counterexamples (CE-A001 through CE-A007) must remain active as falsification boundaries.
*   The TS4 review must not attempt to "solve" or "discharge" these counterexamples globally.
*   Failure to preserve counterexample pressure will result in an immediate gate block.

## 6. Failure Boundary Preservation Requirements
*   Failure signatures (e.g., `ERR_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`, `BRANCH_AMBIGUITY`) must be maintained as first-class structural descriptors.
*   The review must verify that local logic does not collapse or erase these failure modes.

## 7. Cross-Mechanism Limitation Requirements
*   The review must explicitly acknowledge the implementation-sensitivity of the lemma.
*   Mechanism-specific divergence hotspots (e.g., in ED-A006) must remain formally recorded and bounded.

## 8. Governance Constraints
*   **Theorem Status**: MT-LAW-A remains **NOT_PROVEN**.
*   **Scope Status**: **STRICTLY_LOCAL_RESTRICTED_DOMAIN**.
*   **Physics Status**: **NON_PHYSICAL_ANALOG_MODEL**.
*   **Promotion Block**: Promotion to Technical Stage 5 (TS5) or beyond is strictly prohibited.

## 9. Forbidden Escalations
The following actions are **strictly forbidden** during the TS4 review process:
*   Claiming universal persistence.
*   Claiming global mathematical closure.
*   Claiming empirical/physical validation of the lemma.
*   Discharging global counterexample obligations using local restricted-domain logic.
*   Erasing or bypassing mandatory open blockers.

## 10. Review Outcome Conditions
The following conditions must be satisfied during the review:
*   **RC-A001**: TS4 review may evaluate restricted-domain consistency only.
*   **RC-A002**: TS4 review must preserve all unresolved blockers.
*   **RC-A003**: TS4 review must not discharge global counterexamples.
*   **RC-A004**: TS4 review must not imply theorem completion.
*   **RC-A005**: TS4 review must not imply physical validity.

Allowed outcomes for a TS4 review under this gate are limited to:
*   **TS4_RESTRICTED_DOMAIN_REVIEW_ALLOWED**
*   **TS4_REVIEW_DEFERRED_PENDING_BLOCKERS**
*   **TS4_SCOPE_REFINEMENT_REQUIRED**
*   **TS4_COUNTEREXAMPLE_PRESSURE_REMAINS_HIGH**

Any other outcome, specifically those implying proof or physical mapping, will be rejected by the gate validator.
