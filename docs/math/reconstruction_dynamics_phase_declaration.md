# Reconstruction Dynamics Phase Declaration (MPF-RDYN-001)

## 1. Purpose
Freeze admissible terminology and establish the bounded dynamics phase before introducing evolution or stabilization language.

## 2. Phase Definition
- **Canonical Statement**: The reconstruction dynamics phase permits the formalization of how bounded reconstruction structures evolve, stabilize, and interact as governance-only analog indicators for projection systems.
- **Short Form**: `reconstruction_dynamics := bounded_evolution_only`
- **Non-Escalation Rule**: No reconstruction dynamic or stable state may be treated as ontologically primitive, physical proof, or final closure.

## 5. Governance Rules
- **RD-RULE-001**: Reconstruction dynamics define evolution of accessibility, not identity or source truth.
- **RD-RULE-002**: All dynamics constructs must maintain NON_PHYSICAL and ANALOG_ONLY status.
- **RD-RULE-003**: Stable states (basins) do not imply truth, equilibrium, or ontology identity.
- **RD-RULE-004**: Conflict preservation is mandatory across all dynamic transformations.
- **RD-RULE-005**: Recursive interaction does not imply self-complete closure or feedback identity.
