# Law-019: Local Causality\_app as Accessibility Order Law

## 1. Definition
The **Local Causality\_app as Accessibility Order Law** formalizes local causal structure\_app as an emergent property of admissibility-conditioned accessibility ordering. It defines how influence propagates through continuation events without invoking primitive spacetime\_geometry\_proj or global temporal chronology\_app.

## 2. Formal Statement
Within the recursive continuation framework:

- **Orientation Array**: {-(i)_α}
- **Local Reachability Domain**: $D_A(\alpha)$ (as defined in LAW-018)
- **Local Causality\_app Candidate**: $Cause_A(\alpha, \beta)$ holds if and only if:
  1. $\beta \in D_A(\alpha)$
  2. Admissibility remains defined throughout the continuation path.
  3. Continuation ordering permits transport-compatible influence.
- **Ordering Condition**: $\alpha \prec_A \beta$ is defined locally through recursive continuation density and admissibility-compatible propagation.

### Bounded Causality\_app Clause
Causal\_app accessibility is strictly bounded by finite transport flux ($NavT$), reinforcement stability, admissibility thresholds, and reconstruction accessibility ($\Xi$).

### Non-Global Clause
Local causality\_app does not imply a global total ordering of events, universal determinism, or complete causal closure across the entire orientation array.

### Non-Spacetime Clause
Causal\_app accessibility is a topology-conditioned continuation relation (a "reachability order") rather than a primitive spacetime\_geometry\_proj or light-cone\_analog structure.

## 3. Core Principles
- **Admissibility-Ordered Influence**: Causality\_app is the restriction of continuation possibilities such that later events are conditioned by the admissibility of earlier ones.
- **Reachability Constraint**: If locus $\beta$ is outside the reachable domain $D_A(\alpha)$, no causal\_app influence can be established between them within the current orientation frame.
- **Finite Propagation**: Influence cannot bypass the finite flux limits defined by $NavT$ and CSI density.
- **Local Asymmetry**: Causal\_app ordering $\prec_A$ arises from the inherent asymmetry of recursive continuation and reconstruction.

## 4. Governance & Limits
- **No Physics Claim**: This law is a formal definition within the Mono-Process Framework and does not claim to describe physical causality\_phys in the universe.
- **No Relativistic Equivalence**: Causal\_app accessibility is not claimed to be equivalent to the physical light-cone\_analog of general\_relativity\_analog.
- **No Global Temporal\_app Order**: Causal order is local to reachable domains and does not imply a universal "now" or absolute time\_app.
- **No Spacetime\_proj Primacy**: Spacetime\_proj is treated as a projection of these underlying continuation relations, not the source of causality\_app.

## 5. Failure Modes
- **Global Causality\_app Overclaim**: Assuming local ordering extends to a globally consistent causal structure\_app.
- **Primitive Spacetime\_proj Reintroduction**: Treating the accessibility domain as a primitive geometric light-cone\_analog.
- **Absolute Temporal\_app Order Reintroduction**: Assuming $\prec_A$ implies a global chronological clock\_app.
- **Deterministic Universal Causality\_app Claim**: Treating causal\_app accessibility as a proof of universal determinism.
- **Lightcone\_analog Equivalence Leakage**: Using relativistic terminology to justify framework reachability limits.
- **Unbounded Reachability Assumption**: Neglecting the finite bounds of $NavT$ and admissibility.
