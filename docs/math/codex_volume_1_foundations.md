# Codex Volume 1: Foundations

This volume documents the irreducible primitives and foundational expressions of the Mono-Process Framework.

## 1. The Core Expression
The framework is built upon the principle of strict procedural monism, where every state transition is a continuation event governed by mismatch minimization.

## 2. Primitive Terms
- **ε (Epsilon)**: Mismatch, signal, or pressure driving transition.
- **R (Residue)**: Memory, constraint, or trace persisting across transitions.
- **ρ (Rho)**: Continuation capacity.
- **K (CSI)**: Coupling, reach, or interaction domain.
- **-(i) (Orientation Operator)**: Local orientation-conditioned continuation operator.
- **{-(i)} (Orientation Array)**: Distributed reconciliation topology over local orientation operators (Distinction formalized in LAW-006).
- **D_R (Recursion Density)**: Density of admissible reconciliation events across the orientation array (Formalized in LAW-007).
- **≺ (Ordering Relation)**: Admissibility-preconditioned dependency between reconciliation events (Formalized in LAW-007).
- **α ~_A β (Accessibility Relation)**: Admissibility reachability between loci over the orientation array (Formalized in LAW-008).
- **Reach(α, β) (Reachability Condition)**: Formal condition for locus reachability via orientation-array topology (Formalized in LAW-008).
- **T_app(U) (Apparent Temporal Projection)**: derived bookkeeping projection over ordered reconciliation structure in region U (Formalized in LAW-009).
- **Geom_app(U) (Apparent Geometry Projection)**: derived projection of stabilized reconciliation topology in region U (Formalized in LAW-010).
- **B_U (Reconciliation Basin)**: stabilized region of recurrent reconciliation events across the orientation array (Formalized in LAW-011).
- **C_P (Law-Like Persistence Channel)**: recursively reinforced continuation pathway across the orientation array (Formalized in LAW-012).
- **Fracture(C_P)**: topology-conditioned bifurcation, merge, collapse, or redirection of continuation channels (Formalized in LAW-013).
- **𝒞_U (Channel Family)**: set of active continuation channels within a region U (Formalized in LAW-014).
- **S(C_i) (Selection Pressure)**: operational measure determining the persistence support for a continuation channel (Formalized in LAW-014).
- **H(C_P, n) (Reinforcement History)**: ordered record of recurrence events supporting a continuation channel (Formalized in LAW-015).
- **Mem_app(C_P) (Memory Projection)**: apparent memory of a channel projected from its reinforcement history (Formalized in LAW-015).

## 3. The NOT-Axiom
Exclusion necessity condition: ε ≠ 0. This drives the necessity of process continuation.

## 4. Admissibility Foundations
Admissibility defines the window within which continuation is possible. It is the core constraint on the selection of next states.
