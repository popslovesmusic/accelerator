# Ontology Compression and Projection Review

## 1. Purpose
This document conducts a critical review of the existing conceptual landscape of the Mono-Process Framework to identify opportunities for **Ontological Compression**—the reduction of concepts back into simpler, more fundamental process projections.

## 2. Review of Key Concepts

### 2.1 "Thresholds"
- **Current Status:** Formalized as the Threshold Manifold $\mathcal{M}_S$.
- **Compression Analysis:** Can this be reduced further? No. It is a direct geometric projection of the boundary where the core process can no longer continue under the same admissibility rules. It is already maximally compressed.
- **Verdict:** REMAINS_AS_IS.

### 2.2 "Laws" (LAW001-LAW034)
- **Current Status:** Mapped to core expression aspects.
- **Compression Analysis:** While mapped, the sheer number of "Laws" could imply they are fundamental rules. They should be reframed not as "laws" but as "stable, empirically verified projection dynamics."
- **Recommendation:** In future documentation, begin phasing out "Law" in favor of "Projection Dynamic" (e.g., "Persistence Channel Dynamic" instead of "Lawlike Persistence Channel Law").

### 2.3 "Operators" (δ, Π_A, etc.)
- **Current Status:** Clarified as non-substantial process aspects.
- **Compression Analysis:** This is the correct and most compressed formulation. The distinction between the different operator-aspects is necessary to describe the process's behavior.
- **Verdict:** REMAINS_AS_IS.

## 3. Actionable Recommendations
1. Initiate a documentation-wide refactoring to replace "Law" with "Projection Dynamic" or "Process Dynamic" where appropriate to further guard against reification.
