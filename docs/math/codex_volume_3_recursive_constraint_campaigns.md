# Codex Volume 3: Recursive Constraint Campaigns

This volume tracks the evolution of the Recursive Convergence (RC) program through 31 distinct campaigns.

## 1. RC001 - RC010: Foundational Stability
Initial focus on derivation closure, fixed-point scaffolds, and recurrence basin stability.

## 2. RC011 - RC020: Transport and Selection Refinement
Development of branch explosion limits, selection drift minimization, and interaction between selection and retention.

## 3. RC021 - RC031: Structural Formalization
Mapping of explicit functional forms, nonlocal transport closure limits, and multi-branch composition rules.

## 4. Key Results
- Stabilization of recursive recurrence basins.
- Bounds on selection-induced branching.
- Formalization of residue transport conservation laws.
