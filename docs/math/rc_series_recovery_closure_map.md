# RC-Series Recovery and Closure Map (MPF-DEP-002)

## 1. Purpose
This document performs the **formal recovery and classification** of the interrupted RC001-RC031 recursive constraint campaign chain. It ensures that no high-recursion stability assumption silently propagates from unfinished campaigns into theorem-facing or simulation-facing layers. Each campaign is audited for artifact presence and assigned a closure state.

## 2. Closure Classification Criteria
- **RC-CLEAR**: Full artifact stack present (Registry, Doc, Validator, Result). No unresolved risks.
- **RC-PARTIAL**: Missing one or more operational or validation artifacts.
- **RC-SYMBOLIC**: Conceptual scaffold only; lacks operational testing.
- **RC-BLOCKED**: Activates unresolved blockers (Divergence, Bleed, Composition).
- **RC-REQUIRES-SIMULATION**: Needs targeted SIM-layer stress testing.
- **RC-REQUIRES-RECONCILIATION**: Conflicts with established PF/SIM results.
