# LAW-013: Channel Fracture and Transition Law

## Status
- **ID:** LAW-013
- **Level:** candidate_law_form
- **Basis:** LAW-005, LAW-006, LAW-008, LAW-011, LAW-012

## 1. Informal Statement
Persistent continuation channels are not immutable; they evolve through admissibility-boundary pressure, topology transition, and reconciliation destabilization. Channels may fracture, bifurcate, merge, collapse, or redirect when persistence conditions are exceeded.

## 2. Formal Definitions

### 2.1 Orientation Array
The underlying orientation reconciliation topology is denoted as `{-(i)_α}`.

### 2.2 Continuation Channel
A continuation channel `C_P` as defined in LAW-012.

### 2.3 Fracture Condition
`Fracture(C_P)` may occur when admissibility drift, accessibility instability, or transport divergence exceed declared persistence tolerances.
`Fracture(C_P) \iff \sup_{α∈C_P} drift_A(R_α) > η_{fracture}`

### 2.4 Bifurcation Condition
`Bifurcate(C_P → {C_i})` may occur when multiple admissibility-compatible continuation pathways stabilize simultaneously within a previously singular channel.

### 2.5 Merge Condition
`Merge({C_i} → C_P)` may occur when accessibility structures and reconciliation basins converge under compatible continuation reinforcement.

### 2.6 Collapse Condition
`Collapse(C_P)` occurs when recursive reinforcement fails to preserve bounded persistence, leading to the dissolution of the channel structure.

## 3. Law Statements
- **Non-Static Channels:** Continuation channels are dynamic reconciliation structures, not eternal immutable pathways.
- **Topology-Conditioned Evolution:** Channel evolution is governed by admissibility-boundary pressure and accessibility topology transition.
- **Asymmetric Transition:** Transitions may preserve partial continuity, redirection, or reconstruction asymmetry; they are not necessarily reversible or symmetric.

## 4. Governance Constraints
- **No Deterministic Evolution:** This law does not claim a deterministic evolutionary path for channels; transition is conditioned on local reconciliation recurrence and boundary pressure.
- **No Global Equilibrium:** Channel transition does not imply the achievement of or progression toward a global equilibrium state.
- **No Physics Claim:** This law does not claim to describe physical phase transitions, cosmic evolution, or deterministic thermodynamics.

## 5. Failure Modes to Preserve
- Overclaiming channels as eternal or immutable structures.
- Leakage of global equilibrium or deterministic evolution claims.
- Reintroducing primitive geometry or absolute time.
- Modeling topology transition without grounded admissibility conditions.
- Assuming transitions are symmetric or perfectly reconstructible.
- Physics-level validation claims regarding specific physical phase changes.
