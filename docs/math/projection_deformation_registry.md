# Projection Deformation Topology Registry (MPF-RTOP-005)

## 1. Purpose
Characterize how projections deform under translation, simplification, reduction, or sequentialization pressure.

## 2. Deformation Modes
### 2.1 Simultaneity Flattening
- **Mode ID**: `simultaneity_flattening`
- **Definition**: Loss of concurrent aspect roles in favor of sequential viewing.

### 2.2 Residue Erasure
- **Mode ID**: `residue_erasure`
- **Definition**: Deformation that abstracts away memory or constraint history.

### 2.3 Conflict Smoothing
- **Mode ID**: `conflict_smoothing`
- **Definition**: Artificial reduction of projection tension.

### 2.4 Binary Freezing
- **Mode ID**: `binary_freezing`
- **Definition**: Loss of analog granularity, forcing features into binary states.

### 2.5 Analog Diffusion
- **Mode ID**: `analog_diffusion`
- **Definition**: Loss of discrete boundaries, making distinct aspect roles appear as a single field.

### 2.6 Identity Collapse
- **Mode ID**: `identity_collapse`
- **Definition**: Severe deformation where distinctions are lost and artifact is misread as primitive.

## 3. Risk Classes
- **LOW_DEFORMATION**: Retains critical features and loss-accounting accuracy.
- **TRACE_DISTORTION**: Affects clarity of source-relation link.
- **CONFLICT_ERASURE**: Removes mandatory projection-level tensions.
- **ONTOLOGY_COLLAPSE**: Invites primitive promotion or physical identity claims.

## 4. Governance Status
- **Theorem Status**: NOT_PROVEN
- **Scope Status**: STRICTLY_LOCAL_RESTRICTED_DOMAIN
- **Physics Status**: NON_PHYSICAL_ANALOG_MODEL

## 5. Governance Rules
- **DEF-RULE-001**: Every projection deformation must be categorized by mode and risk class.
- **DEF-RULE-002**: Conflict smoothing deformations trigger automatic `REJECT_ESCALATION`.

## 6. Forbidden Claims
- Deformation smoothing derives physical vacuum states.
- Binary freezing proves literal information theory as source.
- Deformation risk metrics are optional for 'simple' projections.
