# MT-LAW-A: Bounded Continuation Persistence Lemma (Definitional Foundation)

## Purpose
This document establishes the definitional foundation for the **Bounded Continuation Persistence Lemma (MT-LAW-A)**. It formalizes the metrics and constraints required to operationally measure structural persistence within the Mono-Process Framework, specifically under the constraints of finite admissibility budgets and bounded transport flux.

This foundation relies upon:
- **LAW-001**: Explicit delta functional form.

## Persistence Definition
Structural persistence ($P_{survival}$) is defined as the emergent survivability of a continuation structure across recursion depth.
- **Continuity Retention**: The preservation of $Id_A$ across transitions.
- **Reconstruction Survivability**: The maintenance of reconstruction fidelity $F_\Xi$ above threshold $\theta_\Xi$.
- **Topology Accessibility Retention**: The persistence of $Reach(\alpha, \beta)$ within a reconciliation basin.
- **Channel Stability**: The resistance of $C_P$ to fracture or collapse.
- **Perturbation Tolerance**: The capacity to absorb $P_\Delta$ without losing regional coherence.

## Admissibility-Cost Definition
The operational admissibility expenditure ($C_A$) associated with continuation:
- **Transition Burden**: The cost of state transition $\Delta x$.
- **Budget Expenditure**: The consumption of $B_{local}(\alpha)$.
- **Topology Traversal Cost**: The cost of non-local transport via NavT.
- **Perturbation Absorption Load**: The cost of dampening disturbances.

## Local Budget Definition
Finite admissibility budget ($B_{local}(\alpha)$) governs legal continuation:
- **Constraint**: $C_A \le B_{local}(\alpha)$.
- **Effect**: If $C_A > B_{local}(\alpha)$, the continuation event is inadmissible, leading to pruning, delay, or failure.

## Persistence Failure Conditions
A continuation structure fails when:
- **Admissibility Exhaustion**: The admissible window $A_\alpha$ collapses or becomes empty.
- **Budget Saturation**: Local or regional resource budgets are depleted without recovery.
- **Topology Disconnect**: Accessibility relations $\sim_A$ fracture irreversibly.
- **Channel Destabilization**: Cumulative drift exceeds basin tolerance $\eta_B$.
- **Reconstruction Divergence**: Structural loss accumulates until $\Xi$ yields only ambiguity.
- **Irreversible Arbitration Loss**: Competing branches cannot be resolved under budget limits.

## Identity Continuity Constraints
Identity is represented as a **bounded continuity class** rather than primitive sameness.
- **Blocked**: Absolute identity or primitive object persistence.
- **Operational Identity**: Continuity is maintained through history overlap and topology preservation.

## Operational Metrics
- **$P_{survival}$**: Probability or measure of structural retention per recursion step.
- **$C_A$**: Cumulative admissibility units expended.
- **$B_{local}(\alpha)$**: Available capacity units at locus $\alpha$.

- **Bounded Admissible Continuation**: All persistence must be gated by admissibility. The system is admissibility-constrained.
- **Failure-Preserving**: Failures must be recorded, not suppressed.
