# GR-like Projection Domain Registry (MPF-PALG-033)

## 1. Purpose
Define the governed feature space for GR-like projection analogs including continuation, smoothness, propagation, geometry-like persistence, constraint flow, and large-scale coherence without claiming derivation of general relativity.

## 2. Domain Definition
- **Canonical Statement**: GR-like projection domains are projection-derived analog structures emphasizing continuation, smoothness, propagation, geometry-like persistence, and bounded constraint flow.
- **Short Form**: `GR_like := continuation_weighted_projection_domain`
- **Non-Identity Rule**: GR-like projection domains are not general relativity and may not be promoted to physical interpretation.

## 3. Governance Status
- **Theorem Status**: NOT_PROVEN
- **Domain Status**: CANDIDATE_GR_LIKE_PROJECTION_DOMAIN
- **Scope Status**: STRICTLY_LOCAL_RESTRICTED_DOMAIN
- **Physics Status**: NON_PHYSICAL_ANALOG_MODEL

## 4. Governed Feature Classes
- **GRP-001**: `continuation_flow` - Projection emphasis on persistence and continuation across locally constrained domains.
- **GRP-002**: `smoothness_readability` - Projection readability favoring continuous-looking structure after distinction detail is abstracted.
- **GRP-003**: `geometry_like_persistence` - Projection-stabilized persistence structure resembling geometry-like coherence.
- **GRP-004**: `constraint_flow_channeling` - Projection emphasis on bounded redistribution of admissibility constraints.
- **GRP-005**: `local_coherence_surface` - Projection surface over which continuation appears locally coherent.
- **GRP-006**: `propagation_readability` - Projection emphasis on continuation appearing as transport or propagation under local constraints.

## 5. Governance Rules
- **GRP-RULE-001**: GR-like projection structures remain subordinate to ⇔R source relations.
- **GRP-RULE-002**: GR-like structures must declare projection depth and recoverability class.
- **GRP-RULE-003**: GR-like projection agreement cannot be treated as evidence for general relativity.
- **GRP-RULE-004**: Geometry-like persistence remains projection-derived and non-spacetime.
- **GRP-RULE-005**: All GR-like artifacts remain ANALOG_ONLY and NON_PHYSICAL.

## 6. Allowed Uses
- Comparing continuation-heavy projection behavior.
- Analyzing geometry-like persistence as projection-induced structure.
- Modeling bounded constraint flow under local admissibility governance.
- Comparing smoothness-heavy projection structure against QM-like distinction-heavy domains.

## 7. Forbidden Uses
- Claiming GR-like equals general relativity.
- Claiming derivation of spacetime curvature, Einstein field equations, or physical gravitation.
- Treating constraint flow as physical gravitational field dynamics.
- Promoting geometry-like persistence into spacetime ontology.
- Using GR-like projections as proof of reality.
