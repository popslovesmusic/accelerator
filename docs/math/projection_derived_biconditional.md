# Projection-Derived Biconditional (MPF-PALG-016)

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the ordinary logical biconditional ($\iff$ or `iff`) as a **flattened reciprocal projection** of simultaneous recursive aspect-binding ($\iff_R$). In this projection, residue-history, indivisibility, and recursive co-generation are removed to permit reciprocal truth-condition comparison within declared local contexts.

## 2. Projection Rule: Reciprocal Flattening
The biconditional is treated as a derivative convenience rather than a primitive identity or a complete process closure.
- **Projection Form**: $\boxed{ \Pi_{biconditional}(A \iff_R B) \to A \iff B }$
- **Core Insight**: Reciprocal truth-dependency is a reduced shadow of the full, historied co-generation relation.

## 3. Loss Accounting
Projections into the domain of the biconditional are inherently lossy. The following features are explicitly recorded as abstracted or lost:
- **Residue History**: The transformation memory and path dependence are discarded.
- **Recursive Co-Generation**: The mutual generation aspect of the relation is replaced by static truth mapping.
- **Aspect Simultaneity**: The co-present nature of the whole is flattened into independent conditional statements.
- **Indivisibility**: The whole-relation context is reduced to reciprocal parts.

## 4. Usage Rules

### 4.1 Allowed Uses
- Using `iff` as a logical projection convenience for reciprocal comparison.
- Comparing truth-conditions inside a **strictly declared symbolic context**.
- Explaining how ordinary logical structures emerge as shadows of the relational process.

### 4.2 Forbidden Uses
- Treating logical biconditional as equivalent to or replacement for $\iff_R$.
- Treating reciprocal truth as residue-preserving process closure.
- Inferring whole-relation indivisibility or non-separability from ordinary `iff`.
- Using biconditional to claim physical unification or proof of reality.

## 5. Distinction Summary
- **$\iff_R$**: Full process-algebraic relation; indivisible; preserves residue and co-generation.
- **$\iff$ (iff)**: Projection-derived; reciprocal truth only; flattened; abstracts history and whole-context.
