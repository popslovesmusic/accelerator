# Law-025: Persistence Decay and Forgetting Law

## 1. Definition
The **Persistence Decay and Forgetting Law** formalizes the bounded weakening, erosion, and eventual loss of reinforced continuation structures. It defines why persistence is transient and how law-like channels and reconciliation basins fade when they are no longer actively reinforced or when resource budgets and reconstruction fidelity degrade.

## 2. Formal Statement
Within the recursive continuation framework:

- **Orientation Array**: {-(i)_α}
- **Decay Operator Candidate**: $D_\lambda(C_P, n)$ is the operator governing the weakening of a continuation channel $C_P$ over recursion depth $n$ under decay parameter $\lambda$.
- **Reinforcement Erosion Condition**: $Erode(C_P)$ occurs when the reinforcement support $Reinforce(C_P, n+1)$ becomes less than $Reinforce(C_P, n)$, indicating that the rate of active reconciliation recurrence is insufficient to offset intrinsic loss or budget depletion.
- **Forgetting Condition**: $Forget(C_P)$ occurs when the reinforcement history $H(C_P, n)$ falls below the reconstruction accessibility threshold, meaning the channel's prior states are no longer reconstructible from the present structure ($\Xi$).
- **Basin Weakening Condition**: $Weaken(B_U)$ occurs when the admissibility margin, reinforcement inflow, or accessibility coherence within a reconciliation basin declines below the threshold for stable persistence.
- **Transient Law-Like Clause**: Law-like persistence channels are fundamentally temporary or metastable. They are valid within a specific reinforcement regime and are not assumed to be eternal or universally necessary.
- **Non-Primitive Memory Clause**: Forgetting is defined as the loss of accessible reinforcement continuity and reconstruction fidelity. It is not the destruction of a primitive "memory" substance or record.

## 3. Core Principles
- **Persistence as Active Maintenance**: Structure is maintained through active reconciliation and reinforcement; in the absence of active support, structures intrinsically decay.
- **Bounded Reinforcement Lifespan**: Reinforcement history has a finite "depth" beyond which it is lost to recursive compression and reconstruction asymmetry.
- **Budget-Conditioned Decay**: Resource depletion (LAW-021) accelerates the weakening of basins and channels.
- **No Eternal Accumulation**: The framework explicitly blocks the assumption that law-like structures accumulate reinforcement forever without limit.

## 4. Governance & Limits
- **No Physics Claim**: This law defines decay within the Mono-Process Framework and does not claim to describe physical entropy, radioactive decay, or thermodynamic dissipation.
- **No Entropy Equivalence**: While decay resembles dissipation, it is modeled as topology erosion and resource depletion, not the increase of a physical entropy primitive.
- **No Psychological Memory Theory**: "Forgetting" is a structural reconstruction limit and does not map to biological brain function, cognitive psychology, or subjective experience.
- **No Eternal Law-Like Channels**: The reintroduction of eternal, structure-independent "laws of nature" is explicitly blocked.
- **No Primitive Memory**: Memory and residue remain projections of reinforcement history and are not independent substances.

## 5. Failure Modes
- **Eternal Reinforcement Overclaim**: Assuming a channel or basin can persist indefinitely without active reinforcement.
- **Perfect Memory Persistence Overclaim**: Assuming the full reinforcement history of a channel remains eternally reconstructible.
- **Decay as Physical Entropy Leakage**: Treating structural weakening as the second law of thermodynamics.
- **Psychological Memory Claim Leakage**: Mapping $Forget(C_P)$ to human memory loss or cognitive aging.
- **Primitive Memory Reintroduction**: Treating forgetting as the erasure of a memory "record."
- **Law-Like Channel Eternity Overclaim**: Asserting that some channels are universally fixed and immune to decay.
- **Forgetting Without Reconstruction Limit**: Modeling forgetting as a random event rather than a consequence of reconstruction fidelity limits.
- **Physics Claim Leakage**: Citing decay dynamics as a validation of physical dissipation laws.
