# Bounded Topology Reconfiguration (MPF-TOPO-RECONFIG-001)

## 1. Purpose
This document defines how **Restricted Local Topology** regions may reconfigure internally. Topology reconfiguration is an internal bounded rearrangement of local relations, not a global topology transformation. The audit process ensures that locality, admissibility, and failure containment are preserved throughout any rearrangement of local topology primitives.

## 2. Mandatory Status & Scope
All reconfiguration modes and their resulting audit logs MUST be marked with:
- **NOT_PROVEN**: Theorem status is unverified.
- **STRICTLY_LOCAL**: Reconfiguration effects and rules apply only to finite local domains.
- **NON_PHYSICAL_ANALOG_MODEL**: No claims of physical reality, structural transformation of spacetime\_analog, or universal laws are permitted.

## 3. Reconfiguration Modes

### 3.1 local_neighbor_rebinding
- **Definition**: A local topology region changes neighbor relations while preserving finite boundaries.
- **Trigger**: Local stability threshold shift.
- **Restriction**: Must not lead to global neighborhood unification.

### 3.2 bounded_path_rerouting
- **Definition**: A continuation path is rerouted through a different local admissible route.
- **Trigger**: Localized path obstruction or budget shift.
- **Constraint**: Must remain within the current local topology region.

### 3.3 boundary_induced_reconfiguration
- **Definition**: Boundary conditions force a local rearrangement of admissible relations.
- **Trigger**: Admissibility exhaustion or hard_boundary encounter.

### 3.4 corridor_relinking
- **Definition**: A bounded corridor changes its local attachment points while remaining governed by constraints.
- **Trigger**: Neighbor region shift.

### 3.5 orientation_neighbor_reassignment
- **Definition**: Orientation-neighbor relations are reassigned locally without creating a global anchor.
- **Trigger**: Local orientation field fluctuation.

### 3.6 failure_driven_reconfiguration
- **Definition**: A local failure condition causes a contained reconfiguration rather than total collapse.
- **Trigger**: Detection of local topology fragmentation.

### 3.7 admissibility_budget_reallocation
- **Definition**: Local admissibility capacity is redistributed across internal topology relations.
- **Trigger**: Resource contention between local paths.

### 3.8 invalid_global_reconfiguration
- **Definition**: A reconfiguration attempt is rejected because it treats local internal changes as system-wide transformations or physical reality.

## 4. Reconfiguration Audit Requirements
The audit system evaluates each mode based on:
- **Classification**: allowed_local, blocked, invalid, or unresolved.
- **Trigger mapping**: Verifying the internal source of the change.
- **Dependency checks**: Ensuring alignment with boundary and transition registries.
- **Admissibility verification**: Confirming local capacity is preserved or correctly reduced.
- **Failure containment**: Ensuring fractures remain bounded.

## 5. Language Restrictions
The following terms are **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN**:
- "global topology reconfiguration"
- "universal topology rearrangement"
- "physical topology transformation"
- "proof of topology reconfiguration"
- "complete topology rewrite"
- "absolute reconfiguration law"
