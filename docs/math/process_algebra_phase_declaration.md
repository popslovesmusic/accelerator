# Process Algebra Phase Declaration (MPF-PALG-001)

## 1. Purpose
The Process Algebra Phase introduces a restricted symbolic layer for describing recursive continuation relations. It does not replace arithmetic, prove physical claims, or globalize topology. Its role is to define how residue-bound equivalence, exclusion-generated distinction, and recursive polarity may be governed as formal symbolic structures.

## 3. Governance Constraints
To maintain epistemic hygiene, all work in this phase is subject to the following **STRICT** mandates:
- **NOT_PROVEN**: All algebraic relations are provisional and unverified as universal theorems.
- **STRICTLY_LOCAL**: Operations and equivalences apply only to finite, restricted-local domains.
- **NON_PHYSICAL_ANALOG_MODEL**: Algebraic results do not imply physical reality or spacetime geometry.
- **REVIEW_ONLY**: Simulation and formal work support review processes, not final proof.

### 3.1 Forbidden Claims
The following claims are strictly **PROHIBITED**:
- Claims that Process Algebra proves physical reality.
- Claims that Process Algebra replaces or invalidates standard arithmetic.
- Claims that `-1` equals `+1` in ordinary mathematics.
- Claims that `⇔R` proves QM/GR unification.
- Claims that topology flow implies a global manifold.
- Claims that "residue" is a primitive physical substance.
