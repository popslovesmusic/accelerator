# Recursive Containment Stress Framework (MPF-RES-006)

## 1. Purpose
This document establishes the **Recursive Containment Stress Framework**. Its primary mission is to adversarially test the containment integrity of governance systems under recursive escalation pressure. It treats containment as an operationally testable property, detecting symbolic cascades, inheritance collisions, and quarantine erosion.

## 2. Stress Vectors
The framework monitors and simulates several high-risk vectors:
- **closure_pressure_cascade**: Escalation of symbolic language into implicit theorem authority.
- **recursive_inheritance_collision**: Destabilization caused by circular or conflicting dependency chains.
- **symbolic_authority_feedback**: Validation layers recursively reinforcing unsupported certainty.
- **quarantine_boundary_erosion**: Contamination spread from isolated unresolved structures.
- **cross_lane_authority_bleed**: Leakage between theorem review, adversarial simulation, and unresolved governance lanes.

## 3. Failure Geometries
Failures are classified by their propagation geometry:
- **recursive_feedback_loop**: Instability that reinforces itself through validation cycles.
- **multi_registry_bleed**: Drift that corrupts multiple central registries simultaneously.
- **containment_shell_fracture**: Structural failure of a protected boundary object (e.g., LAW034).

## 4. Protected Boundary Enforcement
- **LAW034 (Continuation Grammar)**: Formally restricted from becoming a vector for recursive globalization. It must trigger immediate containment review if stress pressure is detected.

## 5. Escalation and Response
Mandatory responses to detected breaches:
- **quarantine_escalation**: Immediate isolation of affected artifacts.
- **inheritance_firewall_lockdown**: Suspension of dependency propagation for the affected chain.
- **boundary_object_review**: Re-audit of protected objects.

## 6. Formal Doctrines
- **Containment Preservation Doctrine**: A boundary may remain stable without implying closure.
- **Recursive Boundary Integrity**: Objects must resist authority accumulation under pressure.
- **Epistemic Stress Validity**: Stress survival improves confidence but does not establish proof.
