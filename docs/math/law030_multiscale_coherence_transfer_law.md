# Law-030: Multi-Scale Coherence Transfer Law

## 1. Definition
The **Multi-Scale Coherence Transfer Law** defines how locally stabilized continuation structures coordinate across emergent, scale-like organization layers. It formalizes the transfer of coherence upward (local-to-regional reinforcement) and the propagation of constraints downward (regional-to-local admissibility narrowing) without assuming primitive scale hierarchies, global synchronization, or physical scale equivalence.

## 2. Formal Statement
Within the recursive continuation framework:

- **Orientation Array**: {-(i)_α}
- **Scale-Like Layer Candidate**: $S_k$ is an admissibility-bounded organization layer induced by coherent reconciliation structure. These are emergent strata rather than primitive background levels.
- **Coherence Metric Candidate**: $Coh(S_i, S_j) := F(accessibility\_alignment, reinforcement\_overlap, arbitration\_compatibility, finite\_budget\_compatibility, invariant\_preservation)$ measures the alignment between distinct organization layers.
- **Upward Transfer Condition**: $Transfer(S_i \to S_j)$ occurs when local coherence (e.g., from a cluster of stabilization basins) reinforces a higher-order basin, channel, or regime above the persistence threshold.
- **Downward Constraint Condition**: $Constrain(S_j \to S_i)$ occurs when higher-order coherence narrows the set of local admissible continuation options, effectively "guiding" local selection through regional reinforcement feedback.
- **Scale Decoupling Condition**: $Decouple(S_i, S_j)$ occurs when accessibility, budget, perturbation load, or arbitration incompatibility blocks the effective transfer of coherence between layers.
- **Non-Primitive Scale Clause**: Scale-like layers are properties of continuation organization, not irreducible spatial or temporal levels.
- **Global Synchronization Block**: Coherence transfer is local, bounded, and propagation-dependent; it does not imply a universal or global synchronization rule across the entire orientation array.

## 3. Core Principles
- **Emergent Hierarchy**: Layers of organization arise from the recursive stabilization of continuation patterns rather than being a background coordinate property.
- **Bi-Directional Interaction**: Stability at one layer can be both a cause and a consequence of stability at other layers.
- **Admissibility Gating**: All coherence transfer must satisfy local admissibility budgets (LAW-021) and topological invariant preservation (LAW-028).
- **Scale Contingency**: The relationship between "scales" is dynamic and depends on the active reinforcement history and reconstruction fidelity.

## 4. Governance & Limits
- **No Physics Claim**: This law defines coherence transfer within the Mono-Process Framework and does not claim to describe physical scale invariance, renormalization, or structural biology.
- **No Physical Scale Equivalence**: Mapping emergent layers directly to physical atoms, cells, or celestial bodies is explicitly blocked.
- **No Global Synchronization**: The framework does not assume that all loci coordinate simultaneously or that there is a global "clock" or "heartbeat."
- **No Primitive Scale Hierarchy**: Scale is an operational appearance, not an irreducible primitive ontology.
- **No Unbounded Coupling**: Cross-layer interaction is strictly bounded by finite admissibility budgets and transport flux.

## 5. Failure Modes
- **Primitive Scale Hierarchy Reintroduction**: Treating "scale" as an irreducible governing law rather than an emergent property.
- **Global Synchronization Overclaim**: Asserting that coherence transfer results in a unified, synchronized global state.
- **Physical Scale Equivalence Leakage**: Using MPF dynamics to "prove" physical scaling laws.
- **Coherence Without Admissibility**: Modeling transfer as a primitive "force" that bypasses admissibility constraints.
- **Transfer Without Budget Constraint**: Ignoring the cost of maintaining multi-layer coherence.
- **Single Scale Reductionism**: Attempting to explain all behavior through a single organization layer.
- **Unbounded Cross-Scale Coupling**: Modeling interactions that ignore the limits of local accessibility horizons.
- **Physics Claim Leakage**: Citing coherence transfer as a validation of physical emergence theory.
