## 3. Review Findings

### 3.1 Supported Interpretations
- **Aspect Nesting**: Recursive polarity may be provisionally interpreted as an aspect-pattern nested inside the continuation closure relation.

- **Nesting Ambiguity**: Usage of ⇔R at multiple levels may create ambiguity between recursive aspect binding and hierarchical operator stacking.

- **Projection Temptation**: The nested form may tempt analysts into physical or arithmetic interpretations (e.g., hierarchical substructure) beyond current mandates.

## 4. Provisional Constraints
To mitigate risks, the following rules are enforced for nested structures:
- **Simultaneity Rule**: Nested relations must be interpreted as simultaneous aspect-preserving wholes, not ordered execution chains.
- **Non-Associativity Rule**: No associativity or operator flattening may be inferred from nested ⇔R expressions.
- **Non-Transitivity Rule**: $A \iff_R B$ and $B \iff_R C$ do not automatically imply $A \iff_R C$.
- **Non-Hierarchy Rule**: Nested structure must not be interpreted as ontological hierarchy unless separately governed.
