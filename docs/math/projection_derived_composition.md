# Projection-Derived Composition (MPF-PALG-015)

## 1. Purpose
This document defines composition ($\circ$) as an **ordered projection-shadow** of simultaneous recursive aspect-binding ($\iff_R$). In this projection, indivisible co-presence is reduced into a sequential participation structure to support local symbolic chaining and combinability.

## 2. Projection Rule: Ordered Shadowing
Composition is treated as a derivative convenience that imposes an analytical order on the holistic process core.
- **Projection Form**: $\boxed{ \Pi_{compose}(A \iff_R B) \to A \circ B }$
- **Core Insight**: Simultaneous recursive participation is flattened into ordered structural combination only after the whole-relation context is abstracted away.

## 3. Loss Accounting
Projections into the domain of composition are inherently lossy. The following features are explicitly recorded as abstracted or lost:
- **Simultaneous Co-Presence**: The co-present nature of aspects is replaced by sequential participation.
- **Whole-Relation Indivisibility**: The structure is fragmented into combinable parts.
- **Residue-History Depth**: The full transformation memory is often flattened into a symbolic connection.
- **Recursive Co-Generation**: The mutual generation of aspects is lost.

## 4. Usage Rules

### 4.1 Allowed Uses
- Using composition as a projection convenience for local symbolic chaining.
- Representing ordered participation after simultaneity is intentionally abstracted.
- Supporting local analytical combinations under **strictly declared projection contexts**.

### 4.2 Forbidden Uses
- Treating composition ($\circ$) as more primitive or fundamental than residue-bound equivalence ($\iff_R$).
- Assuming composition preserves whole-relation semantics or indivisibility.
- Assuming unrestricted associativity or transitivity across composition chains.
- Treating composition as proof of physical or causal interaction.

## 5. Distinction Summary
- **$\iff_R$**: Full process-algebraic relation; simultaneous; indivisible; recursive.
- **$\circ$**: Projection-derived; ordered; flattened; permits context-bound chaining; abstracts simultaneity.
