# MPF-SIM-006: Cross-Simulation Evidence Atlas

## 1. Purpose
This document performs the **formal documentation** for the cross-simulation evidence atlas (`MPF-SIM-006`). This atlas integrates and compares empirical results from simulation campaigns `MPF-SIM-001` through `MPF-SIM-005` to provide a unified evidentiary base for restricted-local theorem review. It maps collective stability, boundary, and phase-transition behavior into a single governed structure.

## 2. Integration Targets

### 2.1 Stability Evidence Alignment (SIM006-T001)
- **Goal**: Compare basin stability classifications across all campaigns to identify consistent stable regimes.
- **Metric**: Consensus basin classification.

### 2.2 Boundary Failure Convergence (SIM006-T002)
- **Goal**: Identify whether boundary inflation, topology severance, and scope bleed activate consistently across campaigns when stressed.
- **Metric**: Blocker activation convergence.

### 2.3 Metastability Cross-Linking (SIM006-T003)
- **Goal**: Trace metastable and oscillatory cases from initial detection through phase-transition thresholds.
- **Metric**: Instability lineage mapping.

### 2.4 Lambda Persistence Correlation (SIM006-T004)
- **Goal**: Correlate Lambda fixed-point persistence with the broader recursive stability metrics.
- **Metric**: Fixed-point stability correlation.

### 2.5 Proof Eligibility Evidence Mapping (SIM006-T005)
- **Goal**: Map collective evidence to formal proof-eligibility statuses (eligible, blocked, or review_required).
- **Metric**: Evidence-based eligibility class.

## 3. Evidence Classification
- **SIM-EVIDENCE-SUPPORTIVE**: Multiple campaigns align with local stability assumptions. Supports formal review.
- **SIM-EVIDENCE-MIXED**: Campaigns show conflicting or threshold-sensitive behavior. Requires formal reconciliation.
- **SIM-EVIDENCE-BLOCKING**: Campaigns consistently trigger failures or proof ineligibility. Blocks proof use.
- **SIM-EVIDENCE-INCOMPLETE**: Missing or malformed data prevents integration. Requires repair.
