# Unresolved Structure Resolution Queue (MPF-RES-002)

## 1. Purpose
This document performs the **formal routing** of unresolved mathematical structures into distinct governance pathways. Instead of treating all unresolvedness as a generic defect state to be "repaired" or "solved," this queue routes artifacts into specific tracks based on their taxonomy class (MPF-RES-001). This ensures that scope-limited or irreducible features are preserved rather than force-resolved.

## 2. Core Principle
> **Resolution strategy must match unresolved structure type.**

## 3. Routing Tracks

### 3.1 Repair Track (URS-TRACK-REPAIR)
- **Target**: `URS-INCOMPLETE`.
- **Goal**: Achieve operational completion through artifact and validator updates.

### 3.2 Operationalization Track (URS-TRACK-OPERATIONALIZE)
- **Target**: `URS-SYMBOLIC`.
- **Goal**: Convert conceptual scaffolds into testable implementations or move to quarantine.

### 3.3 Stress Test Track (URS-TRACK-STRESS)
- **Target**: `URS-METASTABLE`.
- **Goal**: Map stability margins under high-recursion adversarial simulation.

### 3.4 Deception Analysis Track (URS-TRACK-DECEPTION)
- **Target**: `URS-DECEPTIVE`.
- **Goal**: Isolate latent instabilities and false stability grooves.

### 3.5 Bounded Preservation Track (URS-TRACK-BOUNDED)
- **Target**: `URS-SCOPE-LIMITED`.
- **Goal**: Formally harden the local bounds and block global closure claims.

### 3.6 Irreducible Boundary Track (URS-TRACK-IRREDUCIBLE)
- **Target**: `URS-IRREDUCIBLE`.
- **Goal**: Preserve as a permanent open feature of the model's structural ontology.

### 3.7 Quarantine Integrity Track (URS-TRACK-QUARANTINE)
- **Target**: `URS-QUARANTINED`.
- **Goal**: Monitor isolation integrity and prevent theorem-facing leakage.

## 4. Critical Rules
- **Non-Resolution Rule**: `URS-SCOPE-LIMITED` and `URS-IRREDUCIBLE` structures **may not** be force-resolved into global closure.
- **Incompleteness Guard**: No `URS-INCOMPLETE` artifact may support a proof-segment draft until its repair track is closed.

## 5. Governance Footer
- **Theorem Status**: NOT_PROVEN.
- **Scope Status**: STRICTLY_LOCAL_RESTRICTED_DOMAIN.
- **Physics Status**: NON_PHYSICAL_ANALOG_MODEL.
