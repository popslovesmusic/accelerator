# MT-LAW-A: Bounded Continuation Persistence Restricted-Domain Lemma Candidate

## Purpose
This document establishes the **Restricted-Domain Lemma Candidate** for the **Bounded Continuation Persistence Lemma (MT-LAW-A)**. It defines the specific, guarded conditions under which local persistence is proposed to hold, while explicitly excluding global, physical, and universal claims.

## Restricted Domain Declaration
This candidate applies only within a **strictly local restricted domain**. The boundaries of this domain are defined by the availability of admissibility budget ($B_{local}$) and the preservation of topology accessibility ($T_{access}$). Any transition exceeding these boundaries is explicitly excluded from this candidate.

## Explicit Non-Universality Declaration
This document **does not establish universal persistence**. It **does not establish global closure**, nor does it provide a complete proof of structural stability across the entire orientation array. All findings are local, conditional, and non-physical.

## Candidate Lemma Statement (NOT PROVEN, NOT GLOBAL)
**Informal**: Within a bounded local admissibility domain, continuation persistence may be locally maintained when admissibility cost remains within local budget, topology accessibility remains non-severed, reconstruction divergence remains bounded, and identity is treated only as bounded continuity-class behavior.

**Forbidden Interpretations**:
- This is **not a global theorem**.
- This is **not a universal persistence law**.
- This is **not an empirical physics claim**.
- This is **not a complete proof**.
- This **does not discharge counterexamples**.

## Local Assumptions
- **LA-A001**: Continuation expenditure $C_A$ remains locally bounded.
- **LA-A002**: Topology accessibility remains above the severance threshold.
- **LA-A003**: Reconstruction divergence remains bounded.
- **LA-A004**: Identity is a continuity-class property.

## Required Constraints
- $C_A \le B_{local}(\alpha)$
- $T_{access} > \theta_{access}$
- $R_{divergence} < \theta_{div}$
- $I_{continuity} \in \text{Continuity Class}$

## Excluded Domains
The following are explicitly excluded from this lemma candidate:
- **Global Orientation Array**: No claims about the entire distributed topology.
- **Physical Time-Evolution**: No mapping to physical causality or time.
- **Infinite Budget Regimes**: No assumption of limitless resource availability.
- **Zero-Loss Reconstruction**: No claim of perfect history recovery.
- **Universal Identity**: No assumption of primitive object sameness.
