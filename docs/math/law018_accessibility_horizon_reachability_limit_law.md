# Law-018: Accessibility Horizon and Reachability Limit Law

## 1. Definition
The **Accessibility Horizon and Reachability Limit Law** formalizes the intrinsic limits of continuation influence and reachability within the orientation array. These limits arise from finite admissibility, transport decay, and reconstruction constraints.

## 2. Formal Statement
Within the recursive continuation framework:

- **Orientation Array**: {-(i)_α}
- **Reachability Relation**: $Reach(\alpha, \beta)$
- **Accessibility Horizon**: $H_A(\alpha) := \{ \beta \in \{-(i)_\alpha\} : Reach(\alpha, \beta) \text{ fails under admissibility, finite-flux, decay, or reconstruction constraints} \}$
- **Reachable Domain**: $D_A(\alpha) := \{ \beta : \beta \in CSI(\alpha), ||NavT(\omega_\alpha, \omega_\beta)|| < \infty, \text{ and } \Pi_A \text{ remains defined} \}$

### Decay Condition
Reachability may decay when $W_{CSI}(\alpha, \beta)$, reinforcement support, or admissibility margin falls below declared threshold.

### Reconstruction Limit Clause
Some inaccessible regions may remain observationally compressed or reconstructively ambiguous rather than absolutely absent.

### Non-Spacetime Horizon\_analog Clause
Accessibility horizons\_analog are array-topology limits, not primitive spacetime\_lightcones\_analog or physical\_causal\_horizons\_phys.

## 3. Core Principles
- **Finite Interaction Domain**: Influence does not propagate infinitely or instantaneously across the orientation array.
- **Admissibility Gating**: Regions of low admissibility margin or collapsed $\Pi_A$ form effective horizons\_analog for continuation.
- **Transport Decay**: Finite transport flux ($NavT$) and CSI decay limit the effective "reach" of a locus.
- **Reconstructive Ambiguity**: A horizon\_analog is not necessarily an absolute "edge" but a limit beyond which reconstruction $\Xi$ yields only compressed or ambiguous results.

## 4. Governance & Limits
- **No Physics Claim**: This law does not claim to derive or be equivalent to physical horizons in relativity\_analog or any specific physical theory\_phys.
- **No Spacetime\_lightcones\_analog**: This law does not claim to derive or be equivalent to physical lightcones\_analog in relativity\_analog.
- **No Global Causality**: It defines local reachability limits, not a global causal structure\_app.
- **No Absolute Inaccessibility**: "Inaccessible" means unreachable under current continuation/reconstruction constraints, not non-existent.
- **No Primitive Metric Distance\_obs**: Horizons\_analog are defined by topology and dynamics, not by an absolute spatial coordinate.

## 5. Failure Modes
- **Spacetime\_lightcone\_analog Overclaim**: Treating $H_A(\alpha)$ as a relativistic lightcone\_analog.
- **Global Causality\_app Claim Leakage**: Assuming horizons\_analog imply a globally consistent causal ordering\_app.
- **Unbounded Reachability Assumption**: Assuming influence can propagate across the entire array without limit.
- **Hidden Metric Distance\_obs Reintroduction**: Using "distance" as a primitive to define horizons\_analog.
