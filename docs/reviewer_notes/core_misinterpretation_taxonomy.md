# Reviewer Misinterpretation Taxonomy

## 1. Purpose
This document catalogs the most likely ways reviewers—human or agent—may misread or flatten the core principles of the Mono-Process Framework. It serves as a guide for authors and a diagnostic checklist for the semantic validation scripts.

## 2. Taxonomy of Common Misinterpretations

### 2.1 The Equation-First Reading
- **Misinterpretation:** "(ℰ≠0) ⇔_R δ(ℰ>0) is an equation to be solved for ℰ, δ, or R."
- **Correction:** It is not an equation. It is a formal statement of an inseparable, recursive process relationship.

### 2.2 The Physics-Unification-First Reading
- **Misinterpretation:** "This is an attempt to unify QM and GR with a new master equation."
- **Correction:** This is a framework for modeling process continuation and admissibility. It makes no claims about physical reality, only about the internal consistency of its own procedural models.

### 2.3 The Topology/Geometry-First Reading
- **Misinterpretation:** "The framework is a theory of a pre-existing topology or geometry on which processes run."
- **Correction:** Topology and geometry are **derived projections** of stabilized process. They are effects, not causes. Process is primary.

### 2.4 The Simulation-First Reading
- **Misinterpretation:** "The framework's claims are only as valid as the specific simulation results."
- **Correction:** The simulations are bounded, illustrative examples used to stress-test the logical coherence of the formal laws. The framework's core is the process algebra, not any single computational model.

### 2.5 The Dualism Reading
- **Misinterpretation:** "Distinction and continuation are two separate, interacting forces or substances."
- **Correction:** They are inseparable aspects of a single process, like the north and south poles of a magnet. One cannot exist without the other.
