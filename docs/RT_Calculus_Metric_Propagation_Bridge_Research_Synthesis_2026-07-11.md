# RT Calculus: Metric and Propagation Bridge Research Synthesis

**Date:** 2026-07-11  
**Status:** Research synthesis; no bridge claim promoted  
**Scope:** The current metric hierarchy, distinction density, propagation position, probability/statistics/entropy projections, propagation ceilings, and `RATE_UNIFICATION_001`.

## 1. Executive assessment

The recent discussions have produced a coherent *bridge architecture*, but not yet a metric calculus or a validated propagation unification.

The strongest achievement is the separation of layers:

```text
RT_core
  ↓
Δ evaluation under a declared symmetry reference
  ↓
ordinal mismatch and boundary organization
  ↓
RATE_TYPE_ELIGIBLE(x, φ)
  ↓
DISTINCTION_DENSITY(x, φ) = ρ_D(x, φ)
  ↓
PROPAGATION_POSITION(x, φ) = π_P(x, φ)
  ↓
governed projection operators
  ↓
probability / statistics / entropy / propagation ceiling / speed / measured quantity
```

This ordering blocks a common category error: no metric observable is read directly out of `RT_core`. The metric and physics-like quantities must be produced by explicit, typed, provisional projections.

The strongest unresolved issue is no longer whether such a hierarchy can be stated. It can. The issue is whether the intermediate bridge objects have enough formal structure to generate non-arbitrary metrics and domain propagation laws without importing the quantities they are supposed to explain.

## 2. Current governed position

### 2.1 RT core remains unchanged

The current work preserves the primitive family:

- distinction;
- admissibility;
- continuation;
- organization;
- exclusion.

No metric, probability, entropy, physical speed, or unit system is primitive.

### 2.2 Canonical metric-bridge entry

The canonical entry is:

```text
A|E := Δ(A ⟨S⟩_{r_a} E)
```

`Δ` is a qualitative mismatch/asymmetry evaluator under a declared symmetry-reference coupling. Its governed outputs are ordinal:

```text
NO_EXPRESSED_MISMATCH
LOW_MISMATCH
MODERATE_MISMATCH
HIGH_MISMATCH
ADMISSIBILITY_BREAK
```

Boundary behavior is likewise ordinal and trace-explicit: approach, contact, crossing, retreat, and persistence. This is organizational information, not yet distance, magnitude, topology, or a continuous field.

### 2.3 Rate-type eligibility

`RATE_TYPE_ELIGIBLE(x, φ)` is a gate for bridge testing. Its current requirements include:

- `DOF(x) > 0`;
- coupled `A|E` structure;
- declared reference `φ`;
- ordered intermediate continuation;
- endpoint compatibility;
- detectable non-null change;
- generative exclusion before closure.

Passing this predicate establishes only that a candidate may be tested as rate-like. It does not establish that a rate exists, is unique, is metric, or is conserved.

### 2.4 Distinction density

`DISTINCTION_DENSITY(x, φ)` or `ρ_D(x, φ)` is currently defined as:

> the ordered concentration of admissible non-null distinction relative to a declared `φ`.

The decisive refinement is that density is organizational rather than primitively quotient-based. A numerator/denominator interpretation cannot be presumed before the metric bridge exists.

### 2.5 Propagation position

`PROPAGATION_POSITION(x, φ)` or `π_P(x, φ)` is the organizational position of an admissible continuation within a bounded symmetry gradient. It requires rate-type eligibility and defined distinction density.

It is explicitly not identical to speed, probability, entropy, statistics, or a physical propagation constant.

### 2.6 Current projection doctrine

Probability, statistics, entropy, speed, and propagation ceilings are sibling downstream projections. They may share ancestry without being mutually derivable. In particular, a physics-domain ceiling cannot be justified merely by analogy to a neural, economic, or probability-domain rate.

This supports the coupling/rebinding distinction:

- **Coupling:** the domain quantity is generated through a declared function of governed bridge structure and domain conditions.
- **Rebinding:** an existing external formalism is reinterpreted as a density-derived readout only within a stated limit.

The burden is to prove which operation is being performed.

## 3. Strongest open research questions

### Q1. How can ordinal mismatch support a non-arbitrary metric?

The current chain reaches an ordered mismatch classification, but order alone does not determine interval size, ratio, additivity, or a unique scalar embedding.

The key problem is to state the extra conditions under which repeated `Δ` evaluations admit a metric or pseudometric projection. Candidate conditions may include path composition, identity, reference invariance, monotonicity, separability, and a governed treatment of symmetry-equivalent states.

**Validation target:** exhibit either a representation theorem or a counterexample showing that the present axioms permit incompatible scalar embeddings.

### Q2. What exactly makes `ρ_D` a density rather than an ordering label?

“Ordered concentration” avoids premature quotient language, but concentration still needs an operational discriminator. It must be possible to decide, for two eligible organizations under the same declared reference, whether one has more, less, or incomparable distinction density.

Open subquestions:

- What is conserved or counted, if anything?
- How is the reference window bounded without metric size?
- Is density extensive, intensive, neither, or projection-dependent?
- How does it transform under aggregation and coarse-graining?
- Can distinct organizations have equal `ρ_D` but different propagation behavior?

**Validation target:** define a comparison operator and construct minimal cases for equality, strict order, incomparability, aggregation, and coarse-graining.

### Q3. Can propagation position be defined without smuggling in time or distance?

`π_P` depends on an ordered continuation window. If that order silently means temporal succession or spatial traversal, the bridge becomes circular.

The framework needs a primitive-free account of:

- what makes two continuation states consecutive or ordered;
- what counts as movement within the bounded symmetry gradient;
- whether reversal, stalling, branching, and recurrence are admissible;
- how endpoint compatibility behaves when multiple paths reach the same endpoint.

**Validation target:** define `π_P` on a finite partially ordered continuation structure with no numeric time or distance, then show which propagation claims remain invariant under relabeling.

### Q4. Is `RATE_TYPE_ELIGIBLE` sufficient for a rate, or only necessary?

The current evidence correctly treats eligibility as diagnostic only. A system may satisfy nonzero DOF, coupling, ordering, and endpoint compatibility yet still lack a unique or stable rate-like projection.

**Validation target:** search for eligible countermodels with path dependence, oscillation, branching, hysteresis, or incompatible reference choices. If any exist, define a separate `RATE_WELL_DEFINED` predicate rather than expanding eligibility until it carries the conclusion.

### Q5. What is the exact claim of `RATE_UNIFICATION_001`?

The candidate has oscillated between two readings:

1. **Weak type-unification:** probability gradients and physical propagation ceilings are the same *kind* of downstream object because both arise through distinction-density and propagation-position projections.
2. **Strong mechanism-unification:** a single conserved quantity—distinction read through rate—generates both probability position and the physical `c` projection.

The weak claim may be supportable as a typed architectural result. The strong claim needs conservation, uniqueness, and domain calibration that have not been supplied.

**Validation target:** split `RATE_UNIFICATION_001` into weak and strong candidates and prohibit evidence for one from promoting the other.

### Q6. Is a propagation ceiling derivable from density and floor formation?

The present heuristic is:

```text
domain propagation ceiling
  = projection of basin distinction density
    mediated through distinction-floor formation
```

This is promising but underspecified. A ceiling is a bound, not merely a characteristic rate. The framework must explain why a maximal continuation rate exists, why it is finite, why it is stable within a domain, and what changes it.

**Validation target:** derive a ceiling in a toy RT-compatible model from finite admissibility and floor rules, then attempt to construct a legal model with no ceiling. The discriminating axiom must be made explicit.

### Q7. Is `c` a coupling result or a retrospective rebinding?

To claim a physics coupling, the derivation must reach a propagation-ceiling structure before inserting measured `c`, spacetime geometry, Lorentz symmetry, or physical units. Otherwise the result risks being a relabeling of known physics.

**Validation target:** produce a dimensionless qualitative derivation first. Only afterward define a domain calibration map and test whether the calibrated projection preserves the independently derived structural constraints.

### Q8. How do probability and statistics separate while retaining a common bridge?

The earlier induction correctly distinguishes:

- probability as an admissible realization landscape;
- statistics as accumulated realized organization.

The open obligations remain:

- derive probability from admissibility rather than assume a distribution;
- derive statistics from realized residue/organization;
- establish a common symmetry substrate;
- show which procedural invariants survive both projections.

**Validation target:** build one finite process in which admissible possibilities and realized frequencies diverge, then show how the two projections remain coupled without being identified.

### Q9. What is entropy a projection of?

Entropy is currently downstream of distinction density, but its projection operator is not fixed. Several inequivalent candidates remain possible: admissible multiplicity, uncertainty over continuation, residue dispersion, lost distinguishability under coarse-graining, or distance from the distinction floor.

**Validation target:** define competing entropy candidates and test them against zero-DOF symmetry, reversible organization, irreversible residue accumulation, and coarse-graining. Do not select a candidate merely because it reproduces a familiar formula.

### Q10. How does residue alter metric and propagation behavior?

The non-Markov insight is structurally important:

```text
V_n → δR_n → A_{n+1} → V_{n+1}
```

Yet the exact residue update `R_{k+1} = Ψ(ℰ_k, R_k)` and any decay law remain open. Without them, density and propagation may change historically in ways not captured by a state-only projection.

**Validation target:** define at least two lawful `Ψ` candidates—persistent and decaying—and determine which bridge properties are invariant and which become history-dependent.

### Q11. What does bounded symmetry mean without a hidden percentage scale?

The useful image `<S> — RT — <S>` describes an admissibility window bounded by decoupled symmetry. But “0 to 100” must remain illustrative unless a scale-construction rule is supplied.

**Validation target:** formalize the bounded symmetry gradient first as an order/topological interval candidate, identify the admissibility operator, and specify the return condition to decoupled `0DOF` symmetry without numeric endpoints.

### Q12. Are energy and power definitions structurally adequate for physics projection?

The current qualitative definitions are:

- **Energy:** the capability of an organization to admit continuation.
- **Power:** distinction propagation rate; the rate of admissible continuation.

These are coherent organizational definitions, but they do not yet distinguish stored capability from accessible capability, nor explain how sign, transfer, conservation, work, or physical dimensions emerge.

**Validation target:** test the definitions on dormant capability, blocked continuation, loss, transfer between organizations, and equal-energy/different-power cases before attempting physical rebinding.

## 4. Candidate validation program

### Phase A — Signature and type closure

1. Declare domains and codomains for `Δ`, `RATE_TYPE_ELIGIBLE`, `ρ_D`, `π_P`, the admissibility operator, and each projection operator.
2. Separate predicates, ordinal evaluators, organizational objects, and scalar readouts.
3. Mark every use of `φ`, `⟨S⟩`, residue, floor, basin, endpoint, and continuation window as required, optional, or derived.
4. Reject any definition whose output type already presupposes the target metric.

**Pass condition:** every expression is type-checkable without importing distance, duration, probability, entropy, energy, or speed.

### Phase B — Minimal constructive models

Build small finite models using only states, distinctions, admissible transitions, exclusions, declared references, and ordered continuation relations.

Required fixtures:

- one valid linear continuation;
- one branch;
- one cycle;
- one stalled continuation;
- one zero-DOF symmetry case;
- one endpoint-incompatible case;
- one residue-dependent case;
- one coarse-grained version of a valid case.

**Pass condition:** the predicates classify the fixtures consistently and expose at least one nontrivial counterexample.

### Phase C — Invariance and non-arbitrariness

Test:

- state relabeling invariance;
- admissible reparameterization of `φ`;
- symmetry-reference substitution;
- path refinement and coarsening;
- aggregation and decomposition;
- duplicate representation of the same organization.

**Pass condition:** claimed bridge properties survive the transformations declared irrelevant, while reference-dependent properties change only through declared rules.

### Phase D — Projection isolation

Define probability, statistics, entropy, propagation ceiling, speed, energy, and power projections independently from the common bridge layer.

For each projection record:

- required bridge inputs;
- domain-specific assumptions;
- information discarded;
- invariants preserved;
- whether the operation is coupling or rebinding;
- explicit non-derivability from sibling projections.

**Pass condition:** no projection is used to define its own bridge input, and no sibling-domain analogy functions as evidence.

### Phase E — Failure-seeking campaign

Actively search for:

- eligible systems without a well-defined rate;
- equal density with unequal propagation;
- unequal density with equal propagation;
- multiple ceilings under one domain definition;
- no ceiling despite apparent floor formation;
- entropy candidates that disagree on reversible cases;
- probability projections that cannot normalize without imported measure;
- reference changes that reverse conclusions;
- residue laws that destroy claimed conservation;
- empirically successful rebinding with no generative derivation.

**Pass condition:** failures are classified and used to narrow claims; they are not absorbed by redefining terms after the fact.

### Phase F — Domain calibration

Only after the qualitative structure passes Phases A–E:

1. choose a domain;
2. declare the calibration map and units;
3. fit no more free structure than the qualitative derivation permits;
4. make out-of-sample predictions or retrodictions;
5. compare against simpler null models.

**Pass condition:** the bridge predicts or constrains something not inserted by calibration.

### Phase G — Promotion review

Promotion should require:

- a frozen candidate statement;
- typed definitions;
- constructive examples;
- adversarial countermodels;
- invariance results;
- a claim-specific falsifier;
- separation of architectural, mathematical, and empirical support;
- no modification of `RT_core`;
- explicit documentation of surviving risks.

## 7. Recommended immediate research move

The next work should not begin with `c`, entropy, or a full metric equation. It should begin with a finite countermodel suite for `ρ_D` and `π_P`.

That suite can answer three decisive questions at low cost:

1. Can distinction density be compared without a hidden metric?
2. Can propagation position be ordered without hidden time or distance?
3. Do rate-type-eligible systems exist that still have no unique rate?

If the answer to the third question is yes—as is likely—then `RATE_WELL_DEFINEDNESS_001` becomes the missing gate between propagation position and any rate projection. That would strengthen the architecture by making an existing logical gap explicit rather than forcing more work into `RATE_TYPE_ELIGIBLE`.

## 8. Bottom line

The bridge program is now structurally credible enough for formal model-building, but not for promotion to a metric calculus or a physical unification claim.

Its strongest current proposition is architectural:

> Metric and propagation observables are governed projections from qualitative organizational structure, mediated by eligibility, distinction density, and propagation position.

Its strongest research vulnerability is generative insufficiency:

> The current definitions may classify the path toward a metric without yet containing enough structure to produce a unique metric, rate, ceiling, or conservation law.

That vulnerability is productive. It identifies the exact purpose of the next validation cycle: determine which additional conditions are genuinely derived from RT organization and which would be external mathematical imports requiring explicit rebinding.
