# Cross-Dataset Pairings (MPF-EVID-CAMP-003)

## 1. Purpose
Pair candidate-support datasets with adversarial or structurally contrasting datasets to verify the specificity of mathematical signatures. These pairings ensure that signatures are not generic consequences of dataset class or dimensionality, but are unique products of the predicted process logic.

## 2. Pairing Classes
The registry mandates specific types of pairings:
- **phase_transition_vs_randomized**: Compare structured transitions against Gaussian noise.
- **coherent_vs_fragmented**: Test for persistence of interaction reach in contrasting networks.
- **ordered_vs_shuffled**: Verify that temporal/spatial order is required for signature detection.

## 3. Governance Rule
Evidence strength is significantly increased when a prediction pass in a support dataset is accompanied by a prediction failure in its paired adversarial dataset.
