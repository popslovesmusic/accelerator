# Evidence Campaign Phase Declaration (MPF-EVID-CAMP-001)

## 1. Purpose
Establish the formal phase for Governed Cross-Dataset Evidence Campaigns. This phase transitions from infrastructure readiness to active execution, where mathematical predictions are subjected to paired adversarial testing across multiple datasets and mechanism classes.

## 2. Core Principle
**Evidence strength is determined more by resistance to counterexamples and null controls than by isolated positive matches.**

## 3. Mandatory Requirements
- **Counterexample Pairing**: Every primary dataset support candidate must be paired with at least one adversarial or null-control dataset.
- **Null Model Execution**: Campaigns must execute pre-declared null models (e.g., shuffle, random topology) to bound signature significance.
- **Negative Result Indexing**: All failed, null, or contradictory results must be indexed in the permanent negative result registry.
- **Cross-Dataset Preference**: Support strength is weighted by reproducibility across diverse, unrelated datasets.

## 4. Hard Constraints
- No campaign without explicit prediction binding.
- No positive-only campaigns.
- No suppression of failed predictions.
- No undeclared preprocessing or exclusion criteria.
- No hidden parameter tuning.
- No ontology confirmation claims.
- No publication without counterexample analysis.
