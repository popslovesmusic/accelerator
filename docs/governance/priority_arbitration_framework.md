# Priority Arbitration Framework (MPF-OPS-ESCALATE-003)

## 1. Purpose
Rank operational issues and escalation triggers by governance severity and oversight urgency. This framework ensures that limited human oversight resources are focused on the most critical threats to the Mono-Process Framework's foundational integrity.

## 2. Priority Levels
- **CRITICAL**: Immediate block. Threatens the core Source Relation or non-separability mandates.
- **HIGH**: Local block. Threatens the validity of a specific theorem package or tool certification.
- **MEDIUM**: Warning. Material disagreement or ambiguity that requires clarification.
- **LOW**: Minor drift or notation inconsistency.
- **INFORMATIONAL**: Updates on progress or resolved low-risk issues.

## 3. Risk Dimensions
Arbitration weights are calculated across five dimensions:
- **Governance Risk**: Potential to violate core mandates.
- **Evidence Risk**: Potential to corrupt or misrepresent empirical findings.
- **Equivalence Risk**: Inconsistency between Python and C++ implementations.
- **Claim Risk**: Potential for inflationary interpretation.
- **Operational Stability Risk**: Potential to cause cascading autonomous failures.
