# Cross-Mechanism Signature Analysis (MPF-EVID-CAMP-006)

## 1. Purpose
Compare the emergence of mathematical signatures across unrelated mechanism classes (e.g., Agent vs. PDE vs. Graph) and diverse data domains. This analysis verifies that signatures are truly "mechanism-independent" invariants of the framework's logic, strengthening the evidence against implementation-specific artifacts.

## 2. Domains of Analysis
The analysis targets the following domains:
- **Phase Transition**: Discontinuities in selection and density.
- **Synchronization**: Multiscale coherence and alignment.
- **Reaction-Diffusion**: Spatial pattern persistence and decay.
- **Network Dynamics**: Locality and transport accessibility.

## 3. Evidence Weighting
Signatures that survive cross-mechanism comparison are weighted significantly higher in the Level C4-C6 evidence ladder. A signature confirmed in multiple domains is classified as `STABLE_INVARIANT`.
